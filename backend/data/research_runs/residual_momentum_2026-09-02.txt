V.1  THE DETERMINISM FLICKER: CAUSE ISOLATED, AND "IDENTICAL" OVERSTATED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The module docstring said the cause "was not conclusively isolated" and left two
candidates open: (a) float non-determinism in np.linalg.lstsq and (b) yfinance
re-serving different adjusted closes. IT IS (b), and the test is direct rather
than inferential: fetching the SAME 168-ticker, 3963-day price panel twice in
succession returns 357,543 differing cells out of 612,016 (58%), maximum
relative difference 1.3e-6. That is six to seven orders of magnitude larger than
double-precision lstsq reordering noise and exactly the size needed to move a
score-panel maximum in its fifth significant figure.

SEPARATELY, the claim that "'byte-identical' is true of every number this family
reports and not true of one range diagnostic" IS TOO STRONG. Across two full
independent re-runs the per-spec Sharpes, DSRs, PSRs, skewness and kurtosis all
move — by up to ~2e-6 absolute on a Sharpe. What is true, and is all that was
ever needed: every reported number is identical at the 3-4 decimals it is
printed to, the ranking of all 18 specs is identical, and every count and the
verdict are identical. This family is reproducible to reported precision but is
NOT bit-reproducible, and cannot be made so while the price half of its inputs
is an unpinned live API and the factor half is pinned in git.

V.5  ONE POINT-IN-TIME IMPERFECTION WAS NOT DISCLOSED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The point-in-time discussion covers the publication LAG thoroughly and is
correct. It says nothing about the publication REVISION. Ken French rebuilds the

39 mutations were applied one at a time to the residual-momentum module and the

  S1 — THE QUALIFYING-MONTH GATE could be narrowed to the `rf` column alone and

  disappears alongside smb and hml and an rf-only gate still passes it — the
  test was insensitive to the property it was named for. The realistic vintage
  failure is PARTIAL coverage, and under it the control and CAPM arms would be
  scored for a month where FF3 silently returns NaN: exactly the benchmark
  timing advantage this family is built to prevent. Pinned by
  test_a_month_missing_only_ONE_factor_is_refused_for_every_arm.

  S2/S3 — THE INDUSTRY-BUCKET ROW LOOKUP WAS COMPLETELY UNPINNED, and this is a
  LOOK-AHEAD. Replacing `bucket_frame.loc[formation_ts]` with
  `bucket_frame.iloc[-1]`, or `frame.index[-1]` with `frame.index[0]`, both
  survived the whole suite, because every bucket fixture in the test file was
  constant over time. The `.iloc[-1]` variant is not hypothetical: the bucket
  frame is the FULL panel, captured in a closure and never sliced by the harness
  (only the signal frame is truncated), so that mutation would neutralize every
  formation against the industry each name ENDS UP in. Pinned by
  test_industry_buckets_are_read_at_the_formation_date_and_nowhere_else, which
  uses a time-varying bucket frame extending past the formation date.

  THE PRODUCTION CODE WAS CORRECT IN ALL THREE CASES. No result changes; what
  changed is that these properties can no longer break silently.

A fourth mutation (n_trials_override 18 -> 6) also survived and was traced to
being a genuine NO-OP: repool_deflated_sharpe overwrites n_trials, sigma_SR, SR0
and the DSR for every result afterwards, and its default is already pinned at 18
by test_repooling_uses_the_dispersion_of_every_spec_not_just_one_arm. Recorded
rather than pinned twice.

The citation was re-verified from scratch against BOTH the Crossref REST API

Journal of Empirical Finance 18(3), 2011, pp. 506-521,

THE FACTOR-MODEL CORRECTION IS REAL, and it was the right call. The build brief
said BHM use a "Carhart-style" (four-factor, momentum-inclusive) regression.
Independent sources confirm they do not: the residuals come from 36-month
rolling regressions of EXCESS stock returns on the FAMA-FRENCH THREE FACTORS
(MKT, SMB, HML), with no UMD term. Including UMD would have regressed the
momentum factor out of a momentum signal, so this was load-bearing rather than
pedantic, exactly as the pre-registration argued.

EVERY OTHER CONSTRUCTION DETAIL THIS FAMILY ADOPTED IS ALSO CONFIRMED against a
source independent of the two the build used: a 36-month rolling estimation
window; ranking on the past 12 months of residual returns EXCLUDING THE MOST
RECENT MONTH (i.e. 11 months); the sum standardized by the standard deviation of
the residuals OVER THAT SAME 11-MONTH PERIOD (not over the 36-month estimation
window — a mutation making that substitution was tried and is killed); and a
zero-investment top-minus-bottom DECILE portfolio, EQUALLY WEIGHTED and
REBALANCED MONTHLY. All four match the code.

ONE CHARACTERISATION TO CORRECT. "Scoring window (Eq. 9) 11 months, most recent
month skipped" describes the right net effect but not the mechanism. Within the
36-month estimation window this family's scoring window is the LAST 11 months,
so the estimation window's most recent month is INCLUDED; the skip is produced
instead by the 45-day publication lag, which pushes the usable score month back
to t-2 or t-3. When it lands on t-2 the scoring window is EXACTLY BHM's
t-12..t-2; when it lands on t-3 it is one month staler. Relatedly, the text
above describes BHM's own skip as "~2" months in one place and "~1-2" in
another; BHM skip ONE month (they form on t-12..t-2), so this family's measured
60-day median data age is about twice their skip, not marginally more. The
direction of the disclosure was right — this family is MORE conservative than
the source paper, never less — but the size of the gap was understated.

in conflict, and the "HONEST NEGATIVE" verdict is NOT about the point-in-time
factor-revision issue or any other methodological defect.

Read directly from residual_momentum_PREREGISTRATION.txt section 6 (committed in
75b9ff4 before the module was written):

  "(i) the best RESIDUAL spec's deflated Sharpe (DSR, computed at n_trials = 18)
   exceeds 0.95 ... The 0.95 bar is the same one the asset-growth and quality
   families used, KEPT IDENTICAL so this family's verdict is comparable to its
   siblings rather than graded on its own curve."

So the bar that produced "HONEST NEGATIVE" is 0.95 — the identical bar every
sibling family carries, the same bar lazy_prices (0.754) and quality_cbop (0.817)
also failed and were nonetheless registered under the separate informal >=0.5
screening floor. A 0.630 is a fail against 0.95 and a pass against 0.5. Both
sentences are true at once, exactly as they are for all four existing
registrations. Hypothesis (a) in the framing of this question is the correct one.

CONSEQUENCE: the "HONEST NEGATIVE" line is NOT a reason to decline. If this
family is declined it must be declined on its own merits, which is what D.2-D.5
do.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D.2  THE POINT-IN-TIME FACTOR-REVISION ISSUE (V.5) IS REAL, IS CORRECTLY
     DISCLOSED, AND IS **NOT** DISQUALIFYING. It is not the reason for this
     decision.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

V.5 records that the committed Fama-French file was built from the 202606 CRSP
database, so a 2015 formation ranks on 2026-revised values of 2012-2015 factor
months. Assessed on its merits rather than deferred to:

  1. IT IS NOT A LOOK-AHEAD IN THE SENSE THAT MATTERS. Look-ahead bias requires
     information about the FORWARD returns being predicted to leak into the
     signal. What leaks here is a better later estimate of PAST factor months.
     The revised value of MKT/SMB/HML in, say, 2013-07 carries no information
     about an individual S&P 500 name's return over 2015-02..2015-03.

  2. THE CHANNEL IS THIRD-ORDER. The factors are value-weighted aggregates of
     thousands of stocks. A revision shifts stock i's fitted value by
     beta_i x delta_factor; it reaches the cross-sectional RANKING only through
     the dispersion of betas times a revision measured in the third decimal of a
     monthly percent, against monthly returns of order 1e-2.

  3. IT IS UNIVERSAL, NOT A DEFECT OF THIS BUILD. Every published paper using
     French's library has it. No vintage archive exists to fix it against. The
     family's own price inputs carry a documented ~1.3e-6 vendor drift (V.1) and
     nobody treats that as disqualifying either.

  ONE CORRECTION TO V.5's OWN REASSURANCE, recorded because this project does not
  let a comforting sentence stand unchecked: V.5 says the revisions are "well
  inside the ~1e-6 price-vendor noise documented in V.1". That comparison is
  BACKWARDS. The third decimal of a monthly percent is ~1e-5, roughly TEN TIMES
  LARGER than the 1.3e-6 relative price drift, not inside it. The conclusion V.5
  draws is still right — both are negligible against monthly returns of ~1e-2 —
  but the specific inequality as written is wrong and should not be quoted.

VERDICT ON THIS POINT: a genuine, minor, correctly-disclosed deviation from
strict point-in-time. It does not make the 0.630 untrustworthy and it would not,
on its own, block registration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D.3  THE ACTUAL REASON FOR DECLINING: THE WINNING SPEC IS NOT BHM'S CONSTRUCT,
     AND THE ADDED CONDITIONING SUPPLIES 83% OF THE SHARPE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT BHM ACTUALLY BUILD, verified from a primary-adjacent source rather than
assumed: Prunier's independent replication (matheo.uliege.be/2268.2/1798), which
this family's own citation block already names as its corroborating source,
describes the residual-momentum construction end to end — monthly re-ranking on
the standardized residual momentum score, equal weighting ("there is no
consistent gain when using other weighting schemes rather than the equally
weighted one"), and "The ranking procedure is repeated every month and portfolios
are consequently also rebalanced on a monthly basis." THERE IS NO INDUSTRY STEP
ANYWHERE IN IT. The word "industry" occurs twice in the entire thesis, both times
in a passing summary of a DIFFERENT paper's index-construction comparison, never
in the BHM construction. BHM residualize on FF3 — market, size, value. Not
industry.

WHAT THIS FAMILY'S CANDIDATE ADDS: rm_ff3_residual_neutral_ls_h21 applies an
industry-median centering that BHM do not use. Matched on arm and hold, from the
table above:

    rm_ff3_residual_ls_h21          (= BHM's construct)   +0.057   DSR 0.279
    rm_ff3_residual_neutral_ls_h21  (the candidate)       +0.326   DSR 0.630
    ------------------------------------------------------------------------
    contributed by industry neutralization                +0.270   = 82.7%

The spec that reproduces Blitz/Huij/Martens reaches Sharpe +0.057 and DSR 0.279 —
nowhere near the 0.5 floor. Five sixths of the candidate's Sharpe comes from a
conditioning the source paper does not apply.

THE PRE-REGISTRATION'S OWN LOGIC IS INVERTED BY THIS RESULT, and that is the
sharpest way to put the problem. Section 5 of the pre-registration designates the
conditioning axis as a CONFOUND CHECK on a raw finding, not as a construct in its
own right:

  "THE LOAD-BEARING CONSEQUENCE: a raw positive CANNOT be reported as this
   family's finding without its industry-neutral counterpart ... agreeing."

and section 6's condition (ii) requires "that spec's INDUSTRY-NEUTRAL counterpart
... also shows a materially positive Sharpe rather than collapsing toward zero."
The whole apparatus presupposes RAW is the finding and NEUTRAL is the audit. What
actually happened is the mirror image: raw is null (+0.057) and the entire result
lives in the audit leg. The pre-registered rule has nothing to say about that
case because it was never contemplated, and it certainly does not bless it.
Applied literally, condition (ii) becomes degenerate — the neutral spec's
"industry-neutral counterpart" is itself.

WHY THIS IS THE si_dtc SITUATION, and where the analogy is imperfect. It is the
same shape: the DSR sits at a spec that does not express the source paper's
construct, and the construct-faithful spec is far below the floor. It is not
identical — si_dtc's winner was CONTAMINATED (it sorted on volume, a different
variable); this candidate's winner is arguably PURIFIED (neutralization removes an
exposure rather than adding one), and industry-adjusted momentum has its own
respectable literature (Moskowitz-Grinblatt 1999, Grundy-Martin 2001). That
difference is real and is why this decision took the longer route. What settles it
is the magnitude: an adjustment that supplies 83% of the measured edge is not a
refinement of the mechanism, it IS the mechanism being measured, and it is not the
mechanism this family pre-registered a hypothesis about.

WHAT THE +0.270 ACTUALLY IS, economically: raw residual-momentum sorts on this
universe took industry bets that LOST money over 2015-2026, and neutralizing
removed them. That is a real portfolio-construction improvement. But its measured
size is set by a handful of large industry cycles in this specific window (the
2015-16 and 2020 energy collapses, the 2021-22 energy boom, the tech run) — a
small number of effectively independent events, NOT 2,929 independent daily draws.
The DSR machinery deflates for multiple testing, sample length, skew and kurtosis.
It does not deflate for the effective independence of the underlying bets, and
here that gap is large and runs against the candidate.

IN THIS FAMILY'S FAVOUR, stated so the record is not one-sided: unlike asset
growth, THE CADENCE IS RIGHT. h21 is BHM's own monthly rebalance, and the
36-month window, 11-month scoring, standardization, decile legs and equal
weighting were all independently re-verified as matching BHM (section V.8 above).
The construct deviation here is on exactly one axis. It is simply the axis that
carries the result.

D.4  TWO INDEPENDENT SECOND-ORDER CONCERNS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Neither would be decisive alone; both point the same way.

  1. THE 0.630 IS THE MOST GENEROUS OF THREE DEFENSIBLE NUMBERS. V.0 confirms the
     robustness figures are exact: DSR 0.630 at the POOLED sigma_SR 0.1232, but
     0.532 at the ff3 arm's OWN sigma_SR 0.1631, and 0.279 when deflated against
     Round C's benchmark (V.4 ERROR 3). The pooling choice was pre-declared and
     is defensible, but the report itself calls the resulting +0.098 a "gift". At
     0.532 this candidate would be the lowest-scoring row ever registered by this
     project, below noa_neutral's 0.563.

  2. COST SENSITIVITY IS ~5x THE SIBLING FAMILIES'. Annualized from the persisted
     totals over 11.62 years: turnover 20.7x/yr and cost drag ~1.04%/yr, against
     asset_growth's 4.26x/yr and ~0.21%/yr. A 21-day hold on a monthly signal
     re-pays the spread twelve times a year. The 5bp one-way assumption is
     defensible for S&P 500 names, but this spec's net Sharpe moves ~5x further
     per basis point of cost error than the families it is being compared to, and
     the 0bp borrow assumption remains a disclosed optimism on top.

D.6  WHAT WOULD REVERSE THIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * BHM's own construct (raw ff3 residual, monthly, deciles, equal-weight)
    clearing the floor on a wider cross-section. ~12 names per decile leg from a
    200-name sample is the binding constraint on this whole family; BHM sort a

    require revisiting the si_dtc rejection.
