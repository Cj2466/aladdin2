COUNTRY EQUITY INDEX VALUE AND MOMENTUM — PRE-REGISTRATION
Written 2026-09-04, BEFORE any panel was built, any Sharpe computed, or any
DSR computed. Nothing in this family has been run. That is what makes the
choices below binding rather than decorative.

0. THE ONE-LINE HYPOTHESIS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cross-sectional momentum and long-horizon reversal among DEVELOPED-MARKET
COUNTRY EQUITY INDEXES, traded through US-listed single-country ETFs, earn a
positive risk-adjusted spread over 2001-2026 — the country-index result of
Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013), tested on a universe this project has
never touched.

1. THE SOURCE, AND WHAT WAS VERIFIED ABOUT IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Asness, Clifford S., Tobias J. Moskowitz, and Lasse Heje Pedersen, "Value and
Momentum Everywhere", Journal of Finance 68(3), June 2013, 929-985. The paper
was FETCHED and read for this pre-registration (from
w4.stern.nyu.edu/facdir/lpederse/papers/ValMomEverywhere.pdf); the figures
below are transcribed from its Table I, Panel B, not recalled.

  Country indices, 01/1978-07/2011, 18 developed markets, EQUAL-WEIGHTED
  terciles ("for nonstock asset classes (Panel B), securities are equal
  weighted in the portfolios"), statistics computed from monthly return series
  and reported annualized:

      momentum P3-P1     8.7%  (t 4.14)   sd 11.9%   Sharpe 0.73   alpha 8.2% (t 4.00)
      value    P3-P1     6.0%  (t 3.45)   sd  9.8%   Sharpe 0.61   alpha 5.9% (t 3.45)
      50/50 combination                              Sharpe 1.16   (t 6.62)
      correlation(value, momentum) = -0.34

  Momentum's definition is MOM2-12, the past 12-month return skipping the most
  recent month; the paper notes this is conservative for non-stock classes
  ("Momentum returns for these asset classes are in fact stronger when we don't
  skip the most recent month").

  Value's definition FOR COUNTRY INDICES is "the previous month's BE/ME ratio
  for the MSCI index of the country". THIS PROJECT CANNOT OBTAIN THAT. See
  section 4's DEVIATION note — it is the single most important honesty point
  in this document and it is stated before any result exists.

  Rebalancing is monthly; the paper reports that "rebalancing back to equal
  weights annually rather than monthly produces similar results."

2. THE PRIOR — WRITTEN BEFORE RESULTS, AND IT IS NOT OPTIMISTIC
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The honest expectation is a NEGATIVE, for three reasons stated now so a
negative cannot later be dressed up as a surprise and a positive cannot be
treated as expected:

 2.1 McLean & Pontiff (2016, JF) measure returns 58% lower after publication.
     AMP published in 2013. Applying that haircut to Sharpe 0.73 gives roughly
     0.31 — BELOW this project's own 0.50 registration-screening floor before
     a line of code is written. If the family lands near 0.3 that is the
     literature behaving exactly as the literature says it behaves, not a
     discovery.

 2.2 Price momentum has already been run and rejected on five universes here:
     Round C large-cap US, the S&P 600 small/mid-cap re-run, the 11-commodity
     ETF basket (rejected as a disguised long-precious-metals bet), G10 FX
     (best DSR 0.34), and crypto. A sixth momentum family is not a fresh
     hypothesis class. What is fresh is the universe.

 2.3 The instrument is not the paper's. AMP trade country index futures and
     swaps in local terms; this family trades unhedged US-listed ETFs, whose
     USD returns embed the currency. Section 6's confound check exists
     precisely because that difference could manufacture or destroy the result.

     Against all three: this is the first universe in the project with enough
     non-overlapping formations to actually resolve a Sharpe near 0.3, and the
     paper's t-statistics here are the strongest of any candidate reviewed on
     2026-09-04. A clean negative on a genuinely new universe is a reportable
     result in its own right and would be the sixth such momentum negative,
     which is itself a cumulative finding worth having.

3. THE UNIVERSE — FIXED NOW, AND ITS BIAS MEASURED NOT WAVED AWAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIFTEEN US-listed ETFs, one per market, fixed before any result:

    EWA Australia    EWO Austria      EWK Belgium      EWC Canada
    EWQ France       EWG Germany      EWH Hong Kong    EWI Italy
    EWJ Japan        EWN Netherlands  EWP Spain        EWD Sweden
    EWL Switzerland  EWU UK           SPY USA
    (fourteen iShares MSCI single-country funds, plus SPY for the US = 15;
     the remaining three of AMP's eighteen are excluded below)

VERIFIED LIVE 2026-09-04, not taken on trust: a yfinance pull returned
continuous daily closes from 1996-03-18 (the WEBS/iShares launch date) to
2026-09-02 for all fourteen iShares names — 7,665 rows each — and from
1993-01-29 for SPY (8,456 rows). Fourteen plus SPY is fifteen markets;
fifteen plus the three exclusions below accounts for all eighteen of AMP's.

THREE OF AMP'S EIGHTEEN ARE EXCLUDED, each for a stated reason:
  * Norway (ENOR, from 2012-01-24) and Denmark (EDEN, from 2012-01-26) start
    16 years into the panel. Including them would make the sibling-Sharpe
    spread that feeds the DSR's sigma_sr measure differing SAMPLES rather than
    differing SIGNALS. Excluded for the same reason, and by the same rule, that
    the commodities family pinned its formations to a common window.
  * Portugal (PGAL) traded 2013-11-12 to 2024-03-05 and then LIQUIDATED:
    Global X announced the closure 2024-01-19, last trading day 2024-02-16,
    liquidation on or about 2024-02-23, one of 19 funds closed in that batch.
    It also only starts in 2013, so it could not have joined the panel anyway.

SURVIVORSHIP, STATED AS A MEASURED FACT RATHER THAN A CAVEAT. This family uses
cross_sectional.fixed_universe_membership, the same as bonds and commodities,
and inherits the residual bias that function's docstring already discloses:
today's liquid ETFs are a list chosen with hindsight, and a fund that had
already closed would never have been written down. PGAL IS THAT BIAS,
OBSERVED, IN THIS EXACT UNIVERSE, 19 MONTHS AGO. The direction is adverse, and it was
MEASURED rather than asserted: over PGAL's entire 2013-11-12 to 2024-03-05 life
its total return was -1.8%, against +24.4% for EWU, +33.2% for EWG and +76.2%
for EWJ over the same span. Portugal is precisely the kind of name a
long-short's SHORT leg would have wanted, so its absence flatters this family.
The magnitude of that flattery is not knowable from free data and will NOT be
estimated or guessed.

SPY IS THE ONE NON-MSCI MEMBER and this is disclosed rather than smoothed over:
the other fourteen track MSCI single-country indexes, SPY tracks the S&P 500.
The iShares MSCI USA fund (EUSA) would be consistent but only starts in 2010,
which would cost 14 years of panel. SPY is used and the index mismatch is
recorded; it is small relative to a 15-name cross-sectional sort but it is not
zero.

4. THE SIGNALS — TWO, FIXED NOW, AND ONE IS AN ADMITTED DEVIATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 4.1 mom_2_12 — PAPER-FAITHFUL. Cumulative return from t-12 months to t-1
     month, skipping the most recent month, exactly AMP's MOM2-12. Rank high
     to low; long the top tercile.

 4.2 ltr_5y — A DECLARED SUBSTITUTE, NOT THE PAPER'S COUNTRY-INDEX MEASURE.
     The negative of the 5-year return, computed as the log of the average
     price 4.5 to 5.5 years ago divided by the current price. THIS IS NOT WHAT
     AMP USE FOR COUNTRY INDICES — they use the MSCI index's BE/ME, a paid
     dataset this project cannot obtain. It IS the measure AMP themselves apply
     to commodities and currencies, and they justify it by citing Fama & French
     (1996) showing "the negative of the past 5-year return generates
     portfolios that are highly correlated with portfolios formed on BE/ME",
     plus their own Internet Appendix result to the same effect.

     WHY THIS IS FLAGGED SO LOUDLY. Mechanism fidelity has now killed four
     candidates in this project — si_dtc, asset_growth, residual_momentum and
     best_ideas_13f — every time by the same shape: a spec that clears the bar
     is not the paper's construct, and the paper's construct does not clear the
     bar. The value leg here is, by construction, not the paper's construct for
     this asset class. It therefore carries a LOWER PRIOR than the momentum
     leg, and IF THE FAMILY'S RESULT IS CARRIED BY THE VALUE LEG RATHER THAN
     THE MOMENTUM LEG, THAT IS EVIDENCE AGAINST REGISTRATION, NOT FOR IT. This
     rule is fixed now, before any number exists.

5. THE PRE-DECLARED GRID — 12 SORTED SPECS, n_trials FIXED AT 15
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    2 signals {mom_2_12, ltr_5y}
  x 3 holding periods {21, 63, 126} trading days, non-overlapping
  x 2 constructions {ls_tercile, rank_weighted}
  = 12 specs

  ls_tercile is AMP's P3-P1: equal-weighted top tercile long against
  equal-weighted bottom tercile short. rank_weighted is AMP's own alternative
  factor construction, weighting each market in proportion to its signal rank.
  Both are in the paper; neither is invented here.

  LEG WEIGHTING IS FIXED AT EQUAL, NOT SWEPT. AMP equal-weight for nonstock
  asset classes, so equal weight is simultaneously the paper-faithful choice
  and the one that halves the trial count. This is the same discipline the
  crypto family used when it fixed weighting at inverse_vol.

  h21 is the paper's own monthly cadence and is THE headline; h63 and h126 are
  slower-cadence robustness siblings, declared in advance, and are ALSO the
  cheaper cadences — so a result that survives only at h21 is a result that
  survives only where costs bite hardest, and will be reported that way.

  LEG SIZE: terciles over 15 names give max(1, int(15/3)) = 5 per leg, disjoint
  at 10 <= 15. This clears cross_sectional.DEFAULT_MIN_NAMES_PER_LEG = 5
  without a waiver — unlike FX and commodities, both of which had to lower it.

  THREE COMBINATION SERIES are reported in addition: the equal-weighted average
  of the mom_2_12 and ltr_5y ls_tercile spread returns at each of the three
  holds — AMP's own 50/50 with no free parameter and no optimization.

  n_trials IS FIXED AT 15 (12 + 3), NOT 12. The combinations introduce no new
  search, but counting them is conservative-only: DSR falls monotonically in
  n_trials, so the larger denominator can only make a positive harder to
  claim. 15 also clears deflated_sharpe.MIN_TRIALS_FOR_DSR = 5, so the DSR
  correction proper computes for every spec. This family's 15 trials are its
  own and are never pooled with any other family's.

6. THE CONFOUND CHECKS — RUN IN-MODULE FOR EVERY SPEC, NOT DEFERRED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The commodities family's +0.908 Sharpe survived its own DSR and died to a
confound regression run afterwards. That check is moved to the front here and
is mandatory for all 15 specs.

 6.1 EQUAL-WEIGHT BASKET BETA. Regress each spec's daily returns on the
     equal-weight return of all 15 ETFs. Report beta and the beta-hedged
     residual Sharpe (y - beta*x, NOT the OLS residual including the
     intercept — the mistake vol_regime_timing.py caught in itself and
     documented, which would read ~0.000 for every strategy ever measured).

 6.2 DOLLAR-FACTOR BETA — the deviation-specific check. These are UNHEDGED
     ETFs, so a country-momentum sort could be a disguised currency bet, and
     this project's FX family already ranks currencies. Regress each spec on a
     USD factor; report beta and the residual Sharpe after hedging BOTH the
     basket and the dollar.

     THE PROXY, chosen now and with its flaw stated: DX-Y.NYB, the ICE Dollar
     Index, verified live 2026-09-04 to cover 1995-01-02 to 2026-09-02 (8,039
     rows) — the whole panel. It is an INDEX, not a traded instrument, so its
     changes are not an investable return and the hedge is diagnostic rather
     than executable. The traded alternative, UUP, was also checked live and
     only begins 2007-03-01, which would leave the first ~6 years of the panel
     unhedged. Both are used: DX-Y.NYB for the full panel as the headline
     check, UUP as a traded cross-check on 2007-2026, and any disagreement
     between them is reported rather than resolved in the family's favour.

 6.3 STATIC-TILT CONTROL. A signal-free book holding the same average
     long/short country weights the strategy holds on average, with all ranking
     logic removed. If that captures most of the headline Sharpe, the result is
     a static country tilt. This is the test that decided commodities.

 6.4 EVENT CONCENTRATION. Per-formation P&L attribution; report the share of
     total gross return contributed by the best two formation blocks, and the
     Sharpe with those two dropped. Commodities had 43.5% in two blocks and the
     Daniel-Moskowitz overlay lost 93% of its edge to six months.

 6.5 NON-OVERLAPPING FORMATION COUNT, reported per spec, on the face of the
     results table. It is the statistic that has mattered most in every family
     here and it will not be buried.

7. COSTS AND FINANCING — DECLARED NOW
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BASE CASE: 10 basis points one-way, applied to |position change|, for every
spec. Chosen before results as a mid-range estimate for US-listed single-
country ETFs: wider than SPY, tighter than the commodity ETFs this project
priced at higher levels. It is an ASSUMPTION, not a measurement.

MANDATORY SENSITIVITY, declared in advance so it cannot be chosen afterwards:
every spec is also reported at 5bp and at 20bp one-way. These are NOT new
trials — identical specs, one assumption varied — and the 10bp arm is THE
result. A BREAKEVEN one-way cost is reported for every spec.

SHORT FINANCING is assumed at 0bp per this project's standing, disclosed
convention, and a BREAKEVEN annual borrow rate is reported for every spec. The
short leg here is large-cap developed-market ETFs, where real borrow is tens of
basis points, so this assumption is milder than it is for single stocks — but
it is still an assumption and is labelled one.

8. PASS / FAIL — FIXED NOW, APPLIED UNCHANGED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 8.1 VALIDATED EDGE: DSR >= 0.95 at n_trials = 15, the same bar every sibling
     family in this project carries. Anything less is not a validated edge and
     will not be called one.

 8.2 FORWARD-VALIDATION REGISTRATION SCREEN — all four required, no trade-offs:
       (i)   best-spec DSR >= 0.50;
       (ii)  the qualifying spec is mom_2_12, the paper-faithful signal (per
             section 4.2 — a result carried by the substitute value leg does
             NOT qualify);
       (iii) the beta-hedged residual Sharpe from checks 6.1 AND 6.2 retains
             at least 50% of the raw Sharpe, and the static-tilt control of 6.3
             captures less than 50% of it;
       (iv)  preservation_score computed and reported for the candidate spec,
             using the existing preservation_score.py, with the daily return
             series persisted — not recovered later by re-running, which is the
             gap that made best_ideas_13f's score incomputable.

 8.3 AN EXPLICIT REGISTER / DECLINE / DEFER LINE IS REQUIRED IN THE RUN
     REPORT, whatever the numbers say — including when the answer is obvious.
     This is the standing recommendation from the 2026-09-04 registration audit
     and this family is the first one expected to honour it at build time.

9. PRE-DECLARED DIAGNOSTICS — REPORTED, NEVER USED TO SELECT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Per-spec: annualized Sharpe, DSR, PSR-vs-zero, n_formations, average names per
leg, turnover, total cost drag, max drawdown, first-half and second-half
Sharpe. Per-family: the full 15-row table sorted by DSR with NOTHING OMITTED,
the 3 cost arms, and the four confound checks of section 6. A circular block
bootstrap with block length equal to the holding period is reported alongside
every spec, because Sharpe is computed on the daily series while the bets are
monthly-to-semiannual.

10. WHAT A NEGATIVE WILL AND WILL NOT CLAIM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WILL: that country-index momentum and long-horizon reversal, traded through
unhedged US-listed single-country ETFs, on 15 developed markets, over roughly
2001-2026, at 10bp one-way, did not clear this project's bars.

WILL NOT: that AMP are wrong. Four differences would remain unresolved by a
negative here and will be listed in the report — the instrument (unhedged ETFs
versus local-currency index futures), the sample (a 25-year window that
excludes their 1978-2000 half), the value measure (a declared substitute), and
the universe size (15 markets versus 18, with the exclusions adverse). A
negative here is evidence about what this project can trade, not about what the
paper found.
