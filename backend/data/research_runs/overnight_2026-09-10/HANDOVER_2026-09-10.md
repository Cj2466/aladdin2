## 2. The two defects found, both fixed

### 2a. A seven-day asset inside a five-day ledger (merged `dae8256`)

68 of the 73 crypto tickers held no row after 2026-09-07 while the ledger recorded them
covered through 2026-09-09. The real, final 09-08 bar existed at the vendor (BTC-USD close
**78438.578125**) and **would never have been re-asked**, because a covered window is not
re-fetched. `cross_sectional_crypto` — a **live** registration — built its panel a whole day
stale, in an asset class with no non-trading days.

Cause: `ROLLING_WINDOW_TOLERANCE_DAYS = 4` exists for weekends and holidays; on a 7-day
symbol `newest_row + 4` runs past the requested end, so the bound shrank nothing. The
2026-09-10 ledger fix was partially defeated by its own tolerance.

Fix: `CONTINUOUS_CALENDAR_TOLERANCE_DAYS = 1`, per ticker. Repair run: 68 windows shrunk
(all crypto, no equity window moved), 68 tickers advanced to 09-08, 68 of 73 now current;
the 5 left behind carry exactly the feed-end dates the module pre-declares.

Proof the equity path is untouched — measured, not argued: the reproducibility check ran on
the **same as-of date** before and after, and **all three equity panels are byte-identical
on every frame**.

2026-09-10 05:25 local) — a fast error, not free-tier hibernation. The keep-alive GitHub Action uses `curl -fsS`, which
fails on a 503, so it has been reporting failure rather than waking anything.

**Branch `disable-heavy-background-runners` (`15f2923`) is an unmerged fix for exactly this
symptom.** Its commit message records the diagnosis from Render's own Events/Metrics tabs:
the backend was OOM-crash-looping every few minutes on the free tier's 512MB with 12
background tasks running. It stops launching the three heaviest — `autonomous_research`,
`autonomous_portfolio` and `event_scanner` — with the reasoning and the re-enable
instructions written into the code.

**I did not merge it.** Merging is one step from pushing, and pushing auto-deploys. That is
your decision, not a side effect of a verification night.
