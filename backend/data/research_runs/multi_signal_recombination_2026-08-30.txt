meaningfully — this is the strongest combined book the project has
measured. It did NOT produce a certifiable combined edge: the book still
trails its own best constituent, both halves of the pre-declared
significance rule fail as decisively as before, and the estimation-risk-
debiased Kelly fraction is still zero. Re-admitting round_c moved the book
closer to its ceiling, not past it. The binding constraint remains exactly
where the 2026-08-29 build located it — upstream, in signals that stay
positive in the same period out of sample — not downstream in the
allocator. Nothing in this result was manufactured to point either way.
