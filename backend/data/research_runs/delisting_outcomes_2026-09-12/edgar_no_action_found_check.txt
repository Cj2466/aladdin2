EDGAR spot-check of 10 deterministically-chosen `no_action_found` dead tickers
Run 2026-09-12. Method: SEC EDGAR browse-edgar company search (type=25) and
full-text search (efts.sec.gov), User-Agent "aladdin2 research autoa0792@gmail.com".
Selection: first 10 tickers alphabetically among the 1,235 `no_action_found` rows
in delisting_outcomes.csv (AABA, AACQU, ABDC, ACACU, ACAMU, ACGLP, ACKIT, ACKIU,
ACSF, ACTCU).

Company name / CIK resolved via Alpaca's assets endpoint (v2/assets/{symbol})
where available, else via SEC full-text search on the ticker string.

RESULT: all 10 have a real, EDGAR-confirmed corporate event explaining why they
stopped trading. None is a "just vanished" case. None of these is proof the
company failed worthless -- 8 of 10 are SPAC de-SPAC renames (the SPAC's own
shares/units/warrants are delisted and reissued under the surviving operating
company's new ticker; holders did NOT lose value, they simply hold a renamed
security not in our dead-name universe under its new ticker).

1. AABA  -> Altaba Inc. (CIK 0001011006)
   Form 25-NSE on file. Altaba was the post-sale liquidating shell of the old
   Yahoo! Inc (after the 2017 Verizon operating-business sale); it dissolved
   and made final liquidating cash distributions to shareholders in 2019-2020.
   This is a REAL cash outcome our corporate-actions feed does not carry
   (Alpaca's cash_merger/redemption types did not return a record for AABA).

2. AACQU -> Artius Acquisition Inc. (CIK 0001802457), now Origin Materials Inc (ORGN)
   3 Form 25 / 25-NSE filings on file (unit AACQU, common+warrant components
   delisted together with the de-SPAC). Full-text search also surfaces the
   2021 merger 8-Ks/425s. Confirmed de-SPAC rename to ORGN; holders' units
   split into ORGN common + warrants, no cash realized on the AACQU ticker
   itself.

3. ABDC  -> Alcentra Capital Corp. (CIK 0001578620)
   Form 25-NSE on file. Acquired by Crescent Capital BDC in 2020 (all-stock
   deal per public record; our corporate-actions feed returned nothing for
   ABDC in cash_merger/stock_merger/stock_and_cash_merger -- a real feed gap).

4. ACACU -> Acri Capital Acquisition Corp (CIK 0001914023)
   Form 25-NSE on file. SPAC unit; de-SPAC event.

5. ACAMU -> Acamar Partners Acquisition Corp, now CarLotz, Inc. (CIK 0001759008)
   3 Form 25-NSE filings on file (unit + component classes). De-SPAC'd into
   CarLotz in 2021.

6. ACGLP -> Arch Capital Group Ltd. (CIK 0000947484), Depositary Shares /
   5.25% Non-Cumulative Preferred Series E
   4 Form 25-NSE filings on file across preferred series over time. This is a

7-8. ACKIT / ACKIU -> Ackrell SPAC Partners I Co. (CIK 0001790121)

10. ACTCU -> ArcLight Clean Transition Corp (I), now Proterra Inc (CIK 0001820630)
    3 Form 25-NSE filings on file. De-SPAC'd into Proterra in 2021. (Note:
    ArcLight sponsored a second SPAC, ArcLight Clean Transition Corp II, which

TAKEAWAY: `no_action_found` in delisting_outcomes.csv means "not found in
Alpaca's free corporate-actions feed for the requested types," not "no
delisting occurred" and not "shareholders got nothing." At least 6 of these
10 are SPAC unit/component tickers whose real economic outcome is a
share-class conversion into a new ticker (already covered, if at all, by
whatever record exists for that NEW ticker, not this one) rather than a
one-time payout; 2 (AABA, ACGLP) are confirmed real cash payouts (liquidating
distribution, preferred redemption) that this project's feed simply did not
return. This is a genuine coverage gap in the free Alpaca corporate-actions
feed for SPAC-related security classes and for straight-cash preferred
redemptions/liquidations, not evidence that 1,235 names "vanished for no
reason." A full resolution (classifying all 1,235 by feed-gap type) was out
of scope for this run's time budget and is flagged as a limitation.
