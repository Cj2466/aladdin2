4. VERDICTS (the frozen section 5 rule, applied) AND WHAT FOLLOWS
================================================================================
ROUND C: EXCLUSION NOT SUPPORTED AS RECORDED. 14/30 specs clear the
combination-eligibility bar under the mid scenario — and under every other
scenario, identically, including the 5bp control and the EDGE-ranked
variant. The recorded "does not survive a realistic cost model" evidence
was an artifact of the uncorrected EDGE model's ~36bp realized charge on a
universe whose defensible one-way cost is ~1-3.5bp. Cost is simply not a
binding constraint for these low-turnover (21-126-day-hold) books at any
defensible level: the entire flat_control -> low_1bp spread of outcomes is
a 0.012-Sharpe shift on the best spec. The family's combination-pool
status should be re-decided WITHOUT the cost-dominated claim; whether to
actually amend HARD_EXCLUSIONS and re-run the combination is the
downstream decision this report explicitly does not make. Not changed by
this verdict: round_c is still not a validated edge (best DSR 0.240,
standard is 0.90) — it is an honest negative, which is exactly the
population the combination thesis exists to test.

PHASE A EXPANDED: EXCLUSION NOT SUPPORTED AS RECORDED — BUT COST-FRAGILE,
UNLIKE ROUND C. The frozen rule fires (9 specs clear under the mid
scenario; the recorded "under the edge_spread run_tag, zero specs clear"
leg is an estimator artifact: at realistic costs the family looks exactly
like its 5bp-control self, not like the 212/212-negative EDGE run). Two
honest qualifiers, both load-bearing:
 (a) The clearing status is fragile within the plausible cost bracket:
     15 -> 9 -> 2 across low -> mid -> high. A reader who believes the
     conservative end of the published spread evidence keeps only two
     volume_climax variants above water. Contrast round_c: 14/14/14/14/14.
 (b) The "noise" half of the recorded reason SURVIVES in full: every DSR
     is ~0 under every scenario (best 0.004), 197/212 patterns are
     negative everywhere, and the positive cluster is one sub-family.
     What failed re-audit is only the claim that REALISTIC costs kill the
     8-9 flat-positive patterns — they do not, at the mid estimate.
Whether an eligibility rule whose bar is "in-sample Sharpe > 0" should
re-admit a family whose own run report calls it cost-dominated noise is a
policy question for the combination module's owner, not a cost question;
this re-audit's finding is narrowly that the COST evidence recorded for
the exclusion does not hold up.
