WHY E[K] CAME BACK AT THE FLOOR, in the estimator's own words: mean silhouette was
0.235, at or below the 0.25 that Kaufman & Rousseeuw call "no
substantial structure found". ONC found no trustworthy cluster structure in the pooled
matrix (481 specs across 28 families) and fell back to carving it into two
blobs. The 25-seed sweep ranged 2..2, with 25 of 25 seeds landing on 2 -- i.e. every seed agreed, so this is a
CONVERGED result and not an unlucky draw.
The estimator's own interpretation string for this run declines to read a bet count
from it at all, and says the raw pooled count of 857 distinct trials is the only honest
multiplicity number for a population with this little structure.

SO THE HONEST BOTTOM LINE IS NOT 'the DSRs were already correct'. It is: this
methodology did not succeed in measuring the pooled denominator, and the pre-existing
per-family DSRs therefore still stand UNCORRECTED and still too generous by an amount
this run did not manage to quantify. The sensitivity lines under each registration
show the size of what is at stake if a defensible denominator is adopted instead.
