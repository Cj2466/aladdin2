  VERDICT: UNRESOLVED
    DECISIVE: the two calibration shapes disagree on the SIGN of the bias 
    at the operating point. The weak-factor shape gives -2.1453 (procedure 
    UNDERSTATES the truth) while the asset-class-block shape gives +4.5177 
    (procedure OVERSTATES it). Since the real residual correlation shape is 
    unknown -- that is precisely what is being estimated -- the bias cannot 
    be signed, let alone corrected. Under the block shape the observed 
    16.9550 inverts to a true breadth BELOW the bottom of the ladder (< 11, 
    a clear FAIL); under the weak-factor shape it inverts to ~20 (a clear 
    PASS). The correction is a property of the assumed shape, not of the 
    estimator.
    NULL-FIDELITY FAILURE: on real data the in-sample residual breadth 
    exceeds the PIT one by +2.0457 (19.0007 vs 16.9550), but at the 
    inverted truth the weak-factor null predicts a gap of only +0.0102. The 
    null does not reproduce the real data's own in-sample/PIT relationship, 
    most plausibly because real factor loadings drift while the null's are 
    constant. A calibration that cannot reproduce a known feature of the 
    real data should not be used to correct it.
    METHOD 2 IS INTERNALLY UNSTABLE: across its split designs the raw 
    disjoint-sample breadth ranges 14.0112 .. 18.7147, straddling the floor 
    of 15.0. Merely reversing which half estimates and which is tested 
    moves the answer from one side of the floor to the other, so Method 2 
    does not by itself settle the gate either.

JUDGMENT CALLS
==============================================================================
  K1_k_equals_6_in_null:
    k=6 removed everywhere and the synthetic truth has exactly 6 factors -- 
    the merged report's RMT-determined headline k, not hand-picked, but it 
    makes the null FAVOURABLE to the procedure (the estimator is asked to 
    find exactly the factors that exist). Kaiser-Guttman said 8 on the real 
    data. Limitation, not corrected.
  K2_gaussian_iid_innovations:
    Gaussian, i.i.d. in time. Real returns are fat-tailed and 
    vol-clustered, which would add estimation noise and therefore MORE bias 
    -- so this choice understates the bias. A Student-t(4) run at the 
    operating point checks that claim rather than asserting it.
  K3_ladder_and_seeds:
    B_true ladder [31.0, 25.0, 20.0, 17.0, 15.0, 13.0, 11.0], 60 
    Monte-Carlo draws per rung. Ordinary choices, not from any source.
  K4_block_size:
    alternating-block design uses 252-day blocks; sensitivity over [63, 
    126, 252, 504] reported.
  K5_linear_interpolation_inversion:
    the calibration curve is inverted by linear interpolation over 7 rungs; 
    the raw ladder is printed so the interpolation can be checked rather 
    than trusted.
  K6_inherited:
    burn_in=504, refit_every=21, correlation PCA and k=6 are held fixed at 
    the merged report's J1/J3/J4 values, because changing them would break 
    comparability with the 16.9550 under audit.
  K7_idiosyncratic_correlation_shape:
    the adopted synthetic idiosyncratic correlation is c*WW'+I rescaled to 
    unit diagonal, with m=10 weak Gaussian loading modes drawn once under 
    seed 20260908 and held FIXED across rungs so only the intensity c 
    varies. This is THIS SCRIPT'S OWN CONSTRUCTION, not a technique quoted 
    from any paper. It replaces a first equicorrelation design that 
    demonstrably FAILED (flat ladder; see 
    design_n1a_equicorrelation_FAILED). The shape still is not the real 
    residual correlation shape -- whether the estimator's bias depends on 
    shape beyond breadth is an OPEN limitation, which is why the 
    contamination diagnostic is reported per rung instead of the shape 
    being assumed adequate.
  K8_shape_robustness_second_ladder:
    a second calibration ladder uses block-diagonal idiosyncratic 
    correlation on the 7 real asset classes, to test whether the correction 
    depends on the SHAPE of residual correlation or only on its breadth. 
    Neither shape is the real residual shape (which is unknown -- that is 
    the whole problem); agreement between two materially different shapes 
    is evidence of robustness, not proof.
  K9_null_fidelity_limitation:
    the null holds factor loadings CONSTANT over time while real loadings 
    drift. The null-fidelity check reports how far the null's 
    in-sample-vs-PIT relationship is from the real data's, because that gap 
    is the visible symptom of this limitation.
