0. THE ONE-PARAGRAPH ANSWER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Daniel & Moskowitz's volatility scaling DOES what their paper says it does, on
this project's own data, in the direction their paper claims: it reduces this
family's maximum drawdown in 6 of 6 base/arm cells and, on the primary base's
better arm, cuts the number of months worse than -5% from 18 to 11 and turns a
left-skewed return distribution (-0.305) into a right-skewed one (+0.353).
Their BEAR-STATE GATE does nothing here, and the reason is not a judgment call:
their indicator fires in 8 of 138 months in this window against 17.7% in their
own, and those 8 months are two episodes. Their full DYNAMIC
weight is worse than useless on this base. And none of it is registrable: the
best eligible spec reaches DSR 0.3869 against a 0.5 floor, its preservation_score
is 0.00000 because its first half loses money, and 93% of its entire Sharpe
advantage over the un-overlaid base comes from 6 months out of 141 — three of
them consecutive.

1. WHAT DANIEL & MOSKOWITZ ACTUALLY BUILD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Daniel, Kent & Tobias J. Moskowitz, "Momentum crashes", Journal of Financial
Economics 122(2), December 2016, pp. 221-247,
doi:10.1016/j.jfineco.2015.12.002. Fetched as the published JFE PDF from the
first author's own site (kentdaniel.net/papers/published/jfe_16.pdf) and
text-extracted for this build. Nothing below is from memory.

THE BEAR-MARKET INDICATOR, verbatim, Table 4 notes p. 230 (repeated identically
in the section 3.2 variable list, p. 228):

    "IB,t-1 is an ex ante bear market indicator that equals one if the
     cumulative CRSP VW index return in the past 24 months is negative and is
     zero otherwise."

Not a drawdown from a peak. Not a moving-average cross. Not an excess return.
The plain cumulative 24-month TOTAL return on the CRSP value-weighted index,
threshold exactly zero.

THE MARKET VARIANCE, Eq. (4), p. 230: "the variance of the daily returns of the
market over the 126 days prior to time t."

THE PANIC STATE is not a third indicator: it is the interaction of the two,
"bear markets and high volatility" (p. 230), entering as IB x sigma^2_m.

THE DYNAMIC WEIGHT, Eq. (6), p. 233:

    w*_{t-1} = ( 1 / (2 lambda) ) * mu_{t-1} / sigma^2_{t-1}

with mu the conditional expected WML return, sigma^2 its conditional variance,
and lambda "a time-invariant scalar that controls the unconditional risk and
return of the dynamic portfolio."

THE MEAN FORECAST, Fig. 7 caption p. 236 — the implementable, out-of-sample one:

    mu_{t-1} = g0_{t-1} + gint_{t-1} * IB,t-1 * sigma^2_m,t-1

with the coefficients "not over the full sample, but rather from a regression
run from the start of our sample (1927:07) up through month t-1". Note the
regressors are dated t-1 and the return t: THE REGRESSION IS PREDICTIVE, NOT
CONTEMPORANEOUS. Section 6 below records that this build got that wrong once.

THE TWO SIMPLER SCALINGS, Table 7 notes p. 236: "cvol is the constant volatility
strategy, in which the WML returns each month are scaled by the realized
volatility of the daily WML returns over the preceding 126 trading days. For the
variance scaled portfolio, the WML returns each month are scaled by the realized
variance". cvol is attributed in the paper to Barroso & Santa-Clara (2015).

NO CAP AND NO FLOOR ON THE WEIGHT: theirs reaches 5.37 and is negative in 82
months of their sample (p. 233).

THEIR OWN TABLE 7, annualized Sharpe over 1934:01-2013:03:

    WML                 0.682
    cvol                1.041
    variance scaled     1.126
    dyn, out-of-sample  1.194
    dyn, in-sample      1.202

READ THAT HONESTLY BEFORE BUILDING ON IT: of the +0.512 improvement from WML to
the full dynamic strategy, +0.359 — SEVENTY PERCENT — is the plain volatility
scaling that uses no bear indicator at all. D&M's headline is the dynamic
strategy; the bulk of their measured gain is Barroso & Santa-Clara's.

AND WHAT THEY CLAIM THE MECHANISM IS, p. 234: "much of the improved performance
of the constant volatility, and especially dynamic strategy, over the static WML
portfolio is the amelioration of big crashes." The claim is CRASH MITIGATION.
That is why max drawdown is a first-class output here and not a footnote.

5. THE GRID, AND WHY THE DENOMINATOR IS 30 AND NOT 12
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3 bases x 4 overlay arms = 12 new specs. n_trials = 30 = the residual-momentum
family's already-run 18 + these 12.

The 18 base Sharpes were computed, persisted and READ before this grid was
designed, and the choice of which three to build on was made with them in view.
That is a search dimension. dd288f9's own section D.6 instructs any follow-up to
"carry these 18 trials into the denominator"; 30 is that instruction applied. The
already-persisted 18 rows are NOT re-deflated to 30 — they were published under a
pre-registration that fixed 18, and rewriting a persisted result to a denominator
chosen afterwards is what this project's append-only convention exists to
prevent. The enlarged denominator lands on the new rows, which is where the
enlarged search actually happened.

sigma_SR is pooled over all 30 Sharpes (0.14721), giving SR0 = 0.30523. Applied
unconditionally, before any number was inspected; the direction of the effect was
not knowable in advance.

THE FOUR ARMS:
    cvol    w = 1/sigma        D&M Table 7 "cvol" (Barroso & Santa-Clara)
    vscale  w = 1/sigma^2      D&M Table 7 "variance scaled"
    bear    w = 0 in bear      AN ABLATION, NOT A D&M CONSTRUCTION
    dyn     w = mu/sigma^2     D&M Eq. 6 + Fig. 7's out-of-sample forecast

`bear` is labelled an ablation everywhere it appears. D&M never propose a binary
gate; IB enters their construction continuously, through mu. The arm exists only
because it is the one way to separate the bear axis from the volatility axis —
they are fused in `dyn`, and a result there could not be attributed to either.
Its gate level is 0 (fully flat), pre-declared, one value, not searched.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. TWO BUGS THIS BUILD FOUND IN ITSELF, BOTH RECORDED RATHER THAN QUIETLY FIXED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Both were found by auditing the code against the paper and against its own
printed intermediates, after a first set of numbers had already been produced.
Both changed the headline. The pre-fix numbers are given so the size is visible.

BUG 1 — THE FORECASTING REGRESSION WAS CONTEMPORANEOUS. D&M's Eq. (4) pairs
month t's WML return with the state at t-1. The first implementation regressed
y[m] on x[m]. That was NOT a look-ahead in the applied weight — x[m] is known at
the end of month m either way — but it estimated "how does WML co-move with the
panic state it is living through" and used it as "how does WML respond to the
state it inherited", which is a different coefficient and not the paper's.
Corrected to y[j] on x[j-1]. Effect, `dyn` arm only: ff3 -0.2001 -> -0.1640,
ff3n +0.0322 -> +0.0597, ctrl -0.0760 -> +0.0212. Pinned by a test built so the
two specifications cannot agree.

BUG 2 — THE WARMUP WAS NOT HOLDING THE BASE. The design says a warmup month
(before 126 days of history exist) holds the un-overlaid base at weight 1.0, so
that base and overlay share an identical day set and every difference between
them is the overlay. The implementation filled the warmup with 1.0 and THEN
multiplied by the normalization constant k — and k is about 0.029 for the
`vscale` arm, so those months were in fact held at ~3% OF THE BASE, i.e. very
nearly flat. The warmup lands on the sample's first seven months, so this
silently deleted whatever happened there. Found by printing the realized monthly
weights and seeing 0.029 where the docstring promised 1.0. Corrected to normalize
first and fill after. THIS ONE FLATTERED THE CANDIDATE AND ITS CORRECTION IS WHAT
MOVED THE HEADLINE:

    dm_ff3_vscale_h21   Sharpe +0.2614 -> +0.2211,  DSR 0.4122 -> 0.3869
    dm_ff3_cvol_h21     Sharpe +0.1706 -> +0.1372,  DSR 0.2977 -> 0.2833

Every number in this report is post-fix.

8. MECHANISM FIDELITY — WHICH AXIS ACTUALLY DID THE WORK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE LOOK-AHEAD PROBE, and it turns out to be a mechanism probe too. Every weight
was recomputed delayed by one FURTHER whole month. Delay can only destroy
information, never add any: an effect that survives is reading something
genuinely persistent, one that collapses was reading something that had to be
acted on immediately. Sharpe INCREMENT over each arm's own base:

                     headline    +1 month
    ctrl_cvol         +0.1097     +0.1249    survives
    ctrl_vscale       +0.1377     +0.1918    survives
    ff3_cvol          +0.1218     +0.1225    survives
    ff3_vscale        +0.2057     +0.2168    survives
    ff3n_cvol         +0.0789     +0.0785    survives
    ff3n_vscale       +0.1045     +0.1278    survives
    ---------------------------------------------------
    ctrl_bear         +0.0798     +0.0097    COLLAPSES
    ff3_bear          +0.0939     -0.0079    COLLAPSES, sign flips
    ff3n_bear         +0.0521     +0.0172    COLLAPSES
    ctrl_dyn          +0.0990     -0.0126    COLLAPSES, sign flips
    ff3_dyn           +0.0528     -0.2452    COLLAPSES hardest in the grid
    ff3n_dyn          +0.0615     -0.0148    COLLAPSES, sign flips

SIX OF SIX VOLATILITY CELLS SURVIVE. SIX OF SIX BEAR-CONDITIONED CELLS COLLAPSE.
That is as clean an attribution as this project has produced: the volatility axis
reads a state that persists for months (volatility clustering, which is exactly
why Barroso & Santa-Clara's scaling works at all); the bear axis is a
timing bet on two episodes and stops working the moment it is a month late.

It also independently rules out look-ahead in the volatility arms — the one bug
that would produce a better-looking result while staying invisible — and it
matches D&M's own Table 7, where 70% of their improvement comes from the
volatility scaling and not from the bear indicator.

WHY `dyn` FAILS, measured rather than asserted. The expanding-window regression
is UNIDENTIFIED for the first 66 of 141 months: no bear month exists until
2020-03, so gint is not estimable and the arm is simply the base until 2020-04.
Once estimable, gint rests on 8 bear months across 2 episodes, and the intercept
g0 is being estimated on a strategy whose mean return is indistinguishable from
zero — it changes sign 3 times for `ff3` over its 78 estimated months, and is
NEGATIVE in 75 of 78 for `ctrl`. A weight proportional to a sign-unstable
intercept is a sign-unstable weight. D&M's dynamic strategy needs a base with a
reliably signed conditional mean and a bear indicator that fires often enough to
identify its interaction coefficient. This sample supplies neither, and the
result is the grid's worst drawdown deterioration on the primary base (-0.4361
against the base's -0.3804).

corr(dyn weight, vscale weight) is +0.166 (ff3), +0.281 (ctrl), +0.361 (ff3n).
THE PRE-REGISTRATION PREDICTED THESE WOULD BE NEAR 1.0 — reasoning that with IB
off in 130 of 138 months, mu would reduce to the intercept and `dyn` would
degenerate into `vscale` times a constant. THAT PREDICTION WAS WRONG and is
recorded as wrong. The reason it was wrong is the sentence above: the intercept
is not a constant, it is a re-estimated and sign-unstable quantity, so `dyn`
diverges from `vscale` rather than collapsing into it.

H1 IS SUPPORTED AND H2 IS NOT. Volatility scaling improved max drawdown in 6 of

BUT THE IMPROVEMENT HAS A SHARP EDGE, AND IT POINTS THE OTHER WAY. The overlay
de-levers into high-volatility regimes and LEVERS UP into calm ones — to 2.55x on
`vscale`, 19 months above 1.5x and 7 above 2.0x. So it is better against slow
bleeds and bad months and WORSE against a single-day gap: the worst day is 28%
deeper than the base's. Kurtosis rises from 6.36 to 10.37. And every levered
month is held under this project's standing 0 bps borrow/financing assumption,
which is least defensible exactly where the book is largest. A strategy that
is safer on 141 monthly observations and more exposed on its worst single day has
not simply "reduced risk"; it has traded one shape of risk for another, and the
shape it took on is the one this project cannot price.

TWO MORE FINDINGS THAT WEIGH AGAINST THE CANDIDATE, both about the PATH rather
than the summary statistic:

  * THE DRAWDOWN COMPARISON IS NOT LIKE FOR LIKE. The base's -0.3804 is a
    77-day terminal event (peak 2026-05-13, trough 2026-07-29 — the last three
    months of the sample). The overlaid spec's -0.3125 is a 2,081-DAY SLOW BLEED
    (peak 2015-01-20, trough 2020-10-01): it is underwater from within two
    weeks of the sample's start until late 2020. The maximum-drawdown number improved because a
    different episode became binding, not because the same episode was made
    shallower. Nearly six years underwater is not a capital-preserving path
    however the summary statistic reads.

  * preservation_score = 0.00000 FOR BOTH ELIGIBLE SPECS, and not because the
    metric is harsh. Their first-half Sharpes are NEGATIVE (-0.1625 vscale,
    -0.2286 cvol) against second halves of +0.6528 and +0.4934, so the stability
    term is 0 by construction. The whole measured edge lives in the sample's
    second half. On the stability-free variant they score 0.02787 and 0.01196,
    against 0.089-0.107 for the four live registrations and 0.0421 for
    noa_neutral — the weakest of them, and the one already recommended for
    removal earlier tonight. The un-overlaid base scores 0.0 too, so the overlay
    did not create this instability; it also did not fix it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. H3: THE BHM-vs-D&M TENSION, REJECTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Blitz/Huij/Martens's abstract claims residual momentum's whole point is that
conventional momentum "exhibit[s] substantial time-varying exposures to the Fama
and French factors [and] these exposures can be reduced by ranking stocks on
residual stock returns". D&M's crash mechanism IS a time-varying market-beta
effect. If BHM are right, D&M's overlay should help residual momentum LESS than
total-return momentum. The pre-registration recorded that prediction.

It is rejected here. The `vscale` Sharpe increment is +0.2057 on the residual
base against +0.1377 on the total-return control, and the drawdown improvement is
larger for the residual base in relative terms too (20.7% of base drawdown vs
4.0%).

STATED AGAINST THAT CONCLUSION, because it is weak: the control's base Sharpe is
NEGATIVE, so comparing "improvement" across bases of opposite sign is shaky
arithmetic, and both increments sit comfortably inside the noise of this sample.
This is recorded as a direction that did not appear, not as a refutation of BHM.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. THE DECISION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DECLINED. NO FORWARD-VALIDATION ROW WAS CREATED. NO ADAPTER MODULE WAS WRITTEN.
NO ENTRY WAS ADDED TO app/main.py's lifespan().

Against the four criteria the pre-registration fixed before the run:

  (i)   DSR >= 0.5 at n_trials = 30.  FAILED. dm_ff3_vscale_h21 reaches 0.3869
        and dm_ff3_cvol_h21 0.2833. This alone settles it.
  (ii)  MECHANISM FIDELITY — the overlay, not the base, supplies the edge.
        PASSED, and passed well. The base contributes +0.0154 of the +0.2211;
        the improvement survives an extra month of delay; it cannot be
        manufactured by choosing a leverage level (Sharpe is invariant to that,
        pinned by a test); and the overlay's own trading cost is charged.
  (iii) THE PAPER'S OWN CLAIM — max drawdown must FALL. PASSED on the headline
        number (-0.3804 -> -0.3125) but with the two serious qualifications in
        section 9: the worst single day is 28% deeper, and the overlaid spec's
        own drawdown is a 2,081-day period underwater rather than a 77-day one.
  (iv)  preservation_score computed and used. COMPUTED: 0.00000 for both, on a
        negative first half. On the stability-free variant, below every live
        registration including the one already recommended for removal.

SO THE UNUSUAL SHAPE OF THIS DECLINE IS WORTH STATING PLAINLY, because it is the
opposite of the three declines that preceded it tonight. asset_growth,
residual_momentum's own top spec and noa_neutral were all declined for MECHANISM
FAILURE — a high DSR sitting on an axis the source paper never used. This one has
the cleanest mechanism story of any candidate this project has tested: the effect
is where the paper says it is, it behaves the way the paper says it behaves, it
survives a delay probe, and its own ablation isolates it to the right axis. It is
declined on STATISTICAL GROUNDS instead: the base it improves is null, the
improved result still misses the floor, the whole gain rests on six months, and
the path it delivers is six years underwater.

WHAT WOULD REVERSE THIS, stated concretely rather than as a gesture:

  * A base worth overlaying. The overlay is a risk transformation, not an alpha
    source: it cannot manufacture an edge that is not in the base. On a base with
    a real Sharpe it might well earn its place — that is what D&M measured, on a
    WML with a Sharpe of 0.682. Applying it to any FUTURE registered strategy of
    this project's is a live and cheap idea and is NOT foreclosed by this
    decline. That is the most useful thing in this report.
  * A window containing more than two bear episodes, which would let the
    bear-gate and dynamic arms be evaluated at all rather than declared
    underpowered in advance.
  * A financing/borrow model. Every conclusion about the levered arms rests on a
    0 bps assumption at up to 2.55x gross notional.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM. It does not claim to have tested Daniel & Moskowitz.
Their sample is 1,035 months of a broad CRSP universe with a 17.7% bear base rate
and a static WML Sharpe of 0.682. This is 138 months of a 200-name large-cap
sample with a 5.8% bear base rate and a base Sharpe of +0.0154. On the one axis
where this sample HAS power — the volatility scaling — the result points the same
way theirs does, and that is recorded as a corroboration of direction and nothing
more. It also does not claim anything about `ff3n_vscale_h21` (DSR 0.6256) or the
other three industry-neutral cells: those were pre-committed as non-registrable
before the run precisely so a good number there could not become a finding, and
per dd288f9 section D.6 an industry-adjusted construct would need its own
pre-registered family grounded in the Moskowitz-Grinblatt / Grundy-Martin
literature, with its own bar. This grid cannot create it and is not used to.

app/services/execution/ contains zero references to this family or to
"residual_momentum" (verified by grep at decision time).
ExecutionControl.trading_halted is untouched and remains True.
