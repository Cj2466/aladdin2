1. THE PROTECTION THE ORIGINAL ROUND ACTUALLY HAD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pre-registration exists to stop a family being assembled from what already
looked good. The substantive version of that protection was present and was
stated at the time. Quoted verbatim from f81a562's own commit message,
written before this recovery and therefore not something this document can
have arranged after the fact:

    "Patterns were chosen from the literature BEFORE any backtest of this
    family was run, explicitly NOT by mining the earlier rounds'
    per-pattern results; the only pre-run measurement was trade FREQUENCY,
    computed performance-blind from fire sequences alone."

And the module docstring, also from f81a562, binds its own constants:

    "Every constant below was fixed BEFORE this family's first backtest
    run -- they are the pre-registration. None was tuned against
    performance."

The design also carried three structural anti-snooping properties that are
checkable in the code rather than taken on trust, and all three still hold in
the recovered module:

  * n_trials is the family's LITERAL size (28) with no post-hoc exclusions
    and no early stopping. The run confirms it: all 28 patterns produced a
    reportable result and n_trials stayed 28.
  * MAX_FAMILY_SIZE = 40 is a declared hard ceiling on the family, so the
    grid could not be grown after seeing results.
  * Every documented anomaly direction is tested against its own mirror
    (continuation vs reversal, long vs short) as a co-equal alternative, so
    no direction is assumed correct.

WHAT IT DID NOT HAVE, SAID WITHOUT SOFTENING: no formal pre-registration
artifact was produced on 2026-08-26. There is no dated, committed document
from before the run that fixes the grid and the decision rule. The commit
message is contemporaneous evidence of intent, and the constants are
inspectable, but that is weaker than what a *_PREREGISTRATION.txt provides,
and this record does not claim otherwise.

5. PRESERVATION SCORE (CLAUDE.md-mandated, computed here for the first time)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This family predates the requirement, so preservation_score had never been
computed for it. It is now computed for every one of the 28 patterns and
stored in each persisted row's full_result_json.

Best spec, high252_breakout_continuation:

    preservation_score            0.0
    preservation_score_no_stab    0.0590

    inputs: cred = DSR at n_local=28 (0.2467) from run_tag
    low_frequency_patterns_recovery_2026-09-06; periods_per_year = 252.

252 is correct and is NOT an oversight about 15-minute bars: the walk-forward
runs on 15-minute bars, but daily_returns_from_bar_equity collapses per-bar
equity to ONE observation per trading day before any Sharpe is taken, so the
series preservation_score sees is daily-cadence (1,416 observations).

preservation_score is 0.0 because credibility is driven by the DSR, which is
0.2467 -- i.e. the score is reporting the same thing the DSR is, through a
different lens, and agreeing with it. The no-stab variant at 0.059 confirms
this is not a stability artifact. Across the whole family the best
preservation_score is 0.034 (preholiday_long); nothing approaches a passing
level.

The 5bp arm reproduces the main screen's 0.6296 exactly, which is the
internal check that the cost-override path measures the same thing the screen
does.

THE AS-RUN COST IS CONSERVATIVE, WHICH IS THE OPPOSITE OF THE USUAL FAILURE
MODE AND SHOULD NOT BE MISREAD AS ONE. engine.py:176 charges
(cost_bps/10_000) x |position change|, i.e. PER SIDE, so 5bp/side is a 10bp
round trip. The 2026-09-05 EDGE correction measured this project's own
corrected one-way half-spread at a 2.00bp median on a 1,830,000-cell S&P 500
panel, corroborated against Hagstromer (JFE 2021) Table 1's 2.53bp median
full effective spread. So this family charged roughly 2.5-4x the corrected
large-cap reality.

WAS THE ~19x SPREAD BUG IN THIS FAMILY? No, and this was checked rather than
assumed. Commit 997b5fe touched borrow_cost.py, spread_estimator.py and the
lazy_prices correction script; it did not touch intraday_patterns.py or
engine.py. INTRADAY_COST_BPS is 5.0 today and was 5.0 at f81a562 -- verified
by diffing both revisions. It is a flat constant on a separate cost path from
the cross-sectional EDGE estimator, so the corrected estimator neither
applied to it nor was bypassed by it.

THE ONE GENUINE OMISSION: borrow on short legs, which this family charges at
zero. Measured upper bound rather than waved away -- the best spec fires 1.87
times/ticker-year and holds 10 days, so it is in the market 7.43% of the
year; at borrow_cost.py's GENERAL_COLLATERAL_BPS_PER_YEAR = 34.0 for
large-caps like these, a leg held short 100% of that time pays 2.53 bp/YEAR.
Against a 0.63 Sharpe that is immaterial, and it moves the result in the
direction that hardens an already-negative verdict.

8. MECHANISM-FIDELITY REVIEW: CORRECTIONS MADE AT RECOVERY TIME
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 12 works cited by the module were re-checked against retrieved sources
rather than from memory. Ten check out exactly on existence, authors, year
and journal/publisher. No invented paper, no wrong journal, no wrong year.
Three findings required changes, all made to the docstring only, none
touching a pattern definition, threshold, hold length, cost constant or
number:

  (a) A REAL MISATTRIBUTION, now corrected. The bollinger3s_daily reversion
      leg was labelled "(Bollinger's reading)". Bollinger's own published
      Rule 6 says the opposite: "A tag of the upper Bollinger Band is NOT
      in-and-of-itself a sell signal. A tag of the lower Bollinger Band is
      NOT in-and-of-itself a buy signal." The book is now cited for the band
      CONSTRUCTION only. The test is unchanged -- both directions were
      already tested co-equally, which is the right treatment for a rule with
      no author-endorsed direction.

  (b) AN OVERREACH, now bounded. "Studies in Tape Reading" (1910) is
      genuinely Wyckoff writing as "Rollo Tape" (confirmed from the scanned
      title page). But "climax" appears twice in the whole text and never as
      buying/selling climax; the book carries a culmination/exhaustion idea,
      while the formalised volume-climax vocabulary is from Wyckoff's 1930s
      course material. Wyckoff and Granville were also cited jointly for both
      readings when Wyckoff supports reversal and Granville confirmation.

  (c) A MISSING DEVIATION DISCLOSURE, now logged -- and this one bears
      directly on the headline. George & Hwang measure NEARNESS to the
      52-week high as a CROSS-SECTIONAL ranking with ~6-month formation and
      holding periods. high252_breakout is a TIME-SERIES EVENT study on a
      single first-crossing held 10 days. The continuation DIRECTION is
      theirs; the construction is not. Since high252_breakout_continuation is
      the best spec in both the original and the reproduction, this matters:
      a positive there would NOT have been a replication of George & Hwang,
      and must not be read as one.

  (d) A SYSTEMATIC OMISSION, now stated, which cuts toward MORE caution.
      Atkins & Dyl report their reversal is SMALLER than the bid-ask spread
      ("consistent with a market that is efficient after transactions costs
      are considered"); Grant/Wolf/Yu report significance "sharply reduced"
      after a bid-ask cost proxy, and studied index FUTURES rather than
      equities; Stivers & Sun define their effect relative to less-optioned
      stocks on S&P 100 names. The docstring cited all three for their
      effect's existence without the cost qualifier. The prior these sources
      actually license is "real but not obviously tradeable net of costs" --
      which makes this family's negative LESS surprising, not more.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW STATUS: the citation audit above was performed by a
separate reviewer that did not write the module. It has NOT been signed off
