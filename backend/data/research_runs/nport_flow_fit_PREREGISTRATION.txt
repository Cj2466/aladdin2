----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE HYPOTHESIS, AND THE SOURCE IT COMES FROM
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Lou, Dong, "A Flow-Based Explanation for Return Predictability", Review of
Financial Studies 25(12), 2012, pp. 3457-3489. The author's own working paper
was fetched live from https://personal.lse.ac.uk/loud/flows.pdf on 2026-09-05
and converted to text (2,230 lines); every equation and coefficient used by
this family was read off that text, never reconstructed from memory.

Mutual funds facing capital flows scale their existing positions roughly
proportionally. Aggregated across all funds holding a stock, that produces
non-informational buying or selling pressure. Lou's flow-induced-trading
measure (Eq.(3)) captures it, and his expected version (Eq.(5)) forecasts it.
Vayanos & Woolley ("An Institutional Theory of Momentum and Reversal", RFS
26(5), 2013, pp. 1087-1145) model the same pressure as the common cause of
short-run momentum and long-run reversal. Neither mechanism is tested here;
only whether the measure sorts returns on this project's universe and window.

    Eq.(3), verbatim:
        FIT_j,t = [sum_i shares_i,j,t-1 * flow_i,t * PSF_i,t-1] / [sum_i shares_i,j,t-1]

    Eq.(5), verbatim:
        E_t[FIT_j] = [sum_i shares_i,j,t * E_t[flow_i] * PSF_i,t] / [sum_i shares_i,j,t]

2. A CORRECTION TO THIS FAMILY'S OWN BRIEF, RECORDED BEFORE THE RUN
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The brief that commissioned this family stated: "Lou then shows FIT positively
forecasts the following year's returns for the stocks with the highest FIT."
THE PAPER SHOWS THE OPPOSITE FOR *FIT*, and this is recorded here rather than
discovered in the results.

Table III Panel B (equal-weighted, decile 10 minus decile 1, monthly):
    Qtr 0 (FORMATION quarter)     +1.73%/mo   (t =  7.77)
    Qtrs 1-4 (FOLLOWING YEAR)     -0.03%/mo   (t = -0.17)
    Qtrs 5-8                      -0.40%/mo   (t = -2.46)
    Qtrs 5-12                     -0.30%/mo   (t = -2.70)

Lou's own prose, verbatim: "the difference in equal-weighted returns between the
top and bottom deciles ranked by FIT is 5.19% (t = 7.77) in the ranking
quarter. While the return spread is indistinguishable from zero in the
following year, it is -7.20% (t = -2.70) in years two and three combined."

So realized FIT's large positive spread is CONTEMPORANEOUS with the quarter
whose flows define it. It is not a forecast and was never claimed to be one.
The paper's forward-looking claim is E[FIT] (Table V Panel A): "+2.52%
(t = 3.96) in the quarter following portfolio formation and 5.28% (t = 2.63) in
the following year", reversing to "-5.67% (t = -2.17) in quarters six to
twelve".

CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS PRE-REGISTRATION: both measures are declared, in one grid,
with one denominator (section 4). Running FIT alone would be running a test the
source itself says must come back flat; running E[FIT] alone would drop the
measure the brief asked for.

3. DATA, AND THE THREE CONSTRAINTS THAT BIND HARDEST
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOURCE: SEC DERA Form N-PORT quarterly bulk data sets, 2019q4 through the
latest published quarter. Tables SUBMISSION, FUND_REPORTED_INFO,
MONTHLY_TOTAL_RETURN and FUND_REPORTED_HOLDING, pulled by HTTP range request
against the live ZIPs and filtered, while streaming, to this project's
universe CUSIPs. Two prior runs established feasibility and the flow definition
and are NOT re-derived: nport_flow_feasibility_2026-09-05.txt and
nport_flow_definition_resolution_2026-09-05.txt.

FLOW DEFINITION, already resolved and adopted here without re-litigation:
Item B.6.a MINUS Item B.6.c (external net flow, excluding reinvested
distributions), scaled by the fund's PRIOR-quarter NET_ASSETS. That is
algebraically Lou's own Eq.(1) estimand under his own stated assumption that
"investors reinvest their dividends and capital appreciation distributions in
the same fund". Measured on 49,705 real fund-quarters at Spearman 0.9726 and
94.01% sign agreement with Eq.(1).

CONSTRAINT 1 — HISTORY. Public N-PORT begins Oct/Nov 2019, and Eq.(3) needs a
prior quarter, so formations begin 2020-04-01. That is ~6.4 years against Lou's
27. This is the single biggest reason to expect low power and it cannot be
fixed with more work.

CONSTRAINT 2 — THE 60-DAY PUBLICATION WALL. SEC Release 33-10231, verbatim:
N-PORT information "will not be made public until 60 days after the end of the
third month of the fund's fiscal quarter." Every panel value is therefore gated
on the real FILING_DATE of every filing that contributed to it. Combined with
section 2, this means realized FIT is doubly untradeable in the formation
quarter: its return spread is contemporaneous AND the data is two months late.

CONSTRAINT 3 — THE MERGER TERM CANNOT BE REPRODUCED. Lou subtracts MGN_i,t,
"the increase in TNA due to fund mergers in quarter t". Form N-PORT Item B.6's
instruction does the opposite, verbatim: "For mergers and other acquisitions,
include in the value of shares sold any transaction in which the Fund acquired
the assets of another investment company", with NO field separating them. A
fund absorbing another fund therefore reads as a large external inflow that was
not an investor decision. This is irreducible noise in the flow measure under
any N-PORT flow definition. It is mitigated only by the |flow| <= 2.0 cap
declared in section 5 and it biases toward LESS measured signal, not more.

UNIVERSE: this project's point-in-time S&P 500 union over the sample window,
the same construction cross_sectional_quality / asset_growth / lazy_prices use,
gated per formation by sp500_membership_history.was_member. The FULL union is
used, not the 200-name seeded sample the EDGAR families draw: those families
subsample to bound per-ticker SEC fetches, and this family has no per-ticker
fetch to bound. Residual survivorship is the standing one and FLATTERS these
results: point-in-time membership makes the ROSTER honest, not the PRICES, and
unresolvable names are disproportionately index leavers.

CUSIP -> TICKER via form13f_provider's dated map built from SEC's own
fails-to-deliver files — already the map cross_sectional_best_ideas uses.

4. THE GRID — 12 SPECS, FIXED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    measure {fit, expected_fit}                 2
  x holding period {63, 126, 252} days          3
  x rank fraction {0.10 decile, 0.20 quintile}  2
  = 12, long_short throughout, magnitude leg-weighting throughout.

n_trials = 12 (n_local), raised inside the harness by
global_effective_n.dsr_n_trials() to the project-wide pooled figure whenever
that is larger. DSR is reported at every N Policy D requires (section 7).

A 21-day hold is EXCLUDED up front: N-PORT holdings refresh quarterly and are
published 60 days late, so a monthly hold re-pays turnover on an essentially
unchanged ranking.

VALUE leg-weighting is EXCLUDED even though Lou reports value-weighted results
alongside equal-weighted ones (Table III Panel C): adding it would be a fourth
searched axis for a robustness point whose published version is not
qualitatively different from the equal-weighted one.

DIRECTION is +1 for both measures: Lou sorts "stocks into deciles based on FIT
in ascending order" and his hedge portfolio "goes long in the top decile and
short in the bottom decile", so the harness's top-is-long convention already
matches the paper and no sign flip is applied.

5. CONSTRUCTION PARAMETERS — ALL FIXED HERE, NONE SEARCHED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PARTIAL SCALING FACTOR (PSF). Lou's Eq.(3) says PSF comes from "columns 1 and 7
of Table II". Column 7's PSF is
0.858 - 21.337*avgOwnershipShare - 51.076*avgEffectiveSpread, and NEITHER
regressor is reconstructible here: avgOwnershipShare needs shares outstanding
for every position in each fund's WHOLE portfolio, and avgEffectiveSpread is
"the effective half bid-ask spread estimated from the Basic Market-Adjusted
model as described in Hasbrouck (2006, 2009)", which this project does not
implement — and the estimator it does have (Ardia-Guidotti-Kroencke EDGE) is
documented as unreliable as a LEVEL in exactly this large-cap regime, which is
what a -51 level loading would require.

ADOPTED: Lou's own PUBLISHED UNIVARIATE coefficients, Table II column 1 for
outflows (0.970, t = 16.82) and column 5 for inflows (0.618, t = 15.78), i.e. a
two-valued constant depending only on the sign of the fund's flow. Sanctioned
by the author, footnote 9, verbatim: "The main results of the paper are not
sensitive to the particular choice of PSF. Using specifications in other
columns of Table II yields similar return patterns."

NOT re-estimated on this project's own panel for the headline, and the reason
is not laziness: the PSF is applied to form portfolios across the same sample
it would be fitted on, so a full-sample re-estimate is in-sample look-ahead
inside a predictive signal, and an expanding-window alternative is dominated by
estimation noise over ~24 usable quarters. Lou reports that a rolling-window
PSF gives "virtually identical results", so the measured upside is small and
the look-ahead risk is not.

INSTEAD, at the cost of zero additional trials: Lou's univariate Eq.(2) IS
re-estimated on this project's own N-PORT panel and REPORTED, and PSF source is
a pre-declared sensitivity arm (section 6).

RECORDED BRIEF ERROR: the brief and the earlier feasibility report both quoted
the inflow PSF as "0.62" citing Lou's sentence "managers invest only 62 cents
out of each dollar of inflow". That sentence describes Table II column 5. Lou's
Eq.(3) cites column 7, whose flow coefficient is 0.858. Different numbers from
different specifications; the paper is unambiguous. This family uses column 5
DELIBERATELY, for the reason above, not by inheriting the brief's error.

EXPECTED FLOW (for E[FIT]). Lou Eq.(4) in the univariate form his Eq.(5) uses:
flow_i,t+1 = b0 + b1*alpha_i,t, with alpha the fund's monthly Carhart
four-factor alpha over the previous twelve months. Lagged flows are excluded
per his footnote 10, verbatim: "I exclude lagged fund flows to forecast future
flows in Equation (5), because lagged flow-induced trading has no predictive
power for stock returns in the subsequent year."

b0 and b1 ARE estimated on this project's own data, with an EXPANDING WINDOW
using only (alpha, realized next-quarter flow) pairs whose outcome filing was
already public at the snapshot date. Unlike the PSF, this quantity is a
FORECAST by construction, and Lou estimates it on his own sample too. His
published Table IV column 1 values (intercept 0.028, slope 4.827, t 9.67) are
reported alongside but not used: a +2.8%-per-quarter average flow is a
1980-2006 fund-industry-growth number, and carrying it into a 2019-2026 sample
of persistently redeeming active funds would put nearly every fund on the
inflow side of the PSF branch, which is factually wrong about this sample.

The four-factor (not three-factor) alpha is required by Lou's footnote 11: he
excludes raw returns "because the flow-based mechanism is also an important
driver of the price momentum effect". A three-factor alpha would leave exactly
that momentum component inside the signal. Ken French's momentum factor is
fetched and committed alongside the FF3 cache.

REFUSAL THRESHOLDS, all fixed here:
  MIN_QUARTER_GAP_DAYS = 80, MAX_QUARTER_GAP_DAYS = 100  consecutive filings
      must be one fiscal quarter apart
  MAX_REPORT_STALENESS_DAYS = 200                        a fund's latest public
      report may be at most this old at a snapshot (92-day quarter + 60-day
      wall = 152, plus room for a late filer)
  MIN_FUND_NET_ASSETS = $10,000,000                      the size floor the
      flow-definition resolution run's own closing instruction demanded
      ("Micro-funds produce flow ratios up to 911x TNA"); it retained 92% of
      that run's panel
  MAX_ABSOLUTE_FLOW = 2.0                                a quarter flow beyond
      +/-200% of lagged net assets is measuring a merger, a seed redemption or
      a reporting error, not investor flow
  MIN_FUNDS_PER_STOCK = 5                                Eq.(3) is an average
      over "the entire mutual fund industry as one giant fund"; an average over
      two funds is not that
  ALPHA_WINDOW_MONTHS = 12                               Lou's "previous year"
  MIN_EXPECTED_FLOW_TRAINING_PAIRS = 200                 below this the
      first-stage forecast is noise

SNAPSHOT CADENCE: the measure is recomputed at each MONTH END from filings
public at that date and carried forward as a STEP series, never interpolated.
Monthly rather than quarterly because fund FISCAL quarters are staggered, so a
new tranche of filings becomes public every month.

SPLIT ADJUSTMENT: every share count is restated onto a common basis using real
per-ticker split history. This is load-bearing, not housekeeping: because fiscal
quarters are staggered, two funds' t-1 dates can straddle a split, and the
share-count weighted average would then silently over-weight whichever fund
reported after it.

HOLDINGS FILTER: Item C.4 UNIT == 'NS' (number of shares) and Item C.7
ASSET_CAT == 'EC' (equity common), long positions only, CUSIP != 999999999.

6. COSTS — INSIDE THE DSR, NOT BESIDE IT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TURNOVER: cost_model="edge_spread" against the CALIBRATED per-ticker half-spread
frame (spread_estimator.build_calibrated_half_spread_frame) — EDGE as a
relative-cost ranker scaling an externally published S&P 500 median level, with
a per-ticker $0.01-tick floor. The same basis cross_sectional_lazy_prices
adopted on 2026-09-05. The raw uncalibrated EDGE frame is ~19x too expensive on
this project's own panel and is not used.

BORROW: financing_bps_per_year = 17.0 = borrow_cost.
financing_bps_for_long_short_book(34.0), i.e. the D'Avolio (2002) /
Beneish-Lee-Nichols (2015) general-collateral rate of 34 bps/yr charged on the
short leg alone. Justified for this universe by D'Avolio p.273 verbatim: "S&P
500 constituents, provided in excess supply by indexing lenders, are almost
always general collateral." This family deliberately does NOT inherit the 0.0
financing every other single-stock equity family here carries; it has no live
registration whose fingerprint a non-zero value would park.

PRE-DECLARED SENSITIVITY ARMS — Sharpe AND DSR recomputed, NO new trials:
  (a) short-leg borrow at {0, 34, 430} bps/yr, the last being D'Avolio Table 3's
      value-weighted mean fee for "specials", an explicit worst case in which
      every short-leg name is hard to borrow;
  (b) PSF source at {Lou published (headline), re-estimated on our own panel,
      perfect scaling PSF == 1}.
A verdict that flips across either ladder is reported as flipping.

8. THE HONEST PRIOR, WRITTEN BEFORE ANY NUMBER EXISTS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOW. Four independent reasons, each stated so that a positive result would have
to survive them:

  (1) Six years of history against Lou's twenty-seven, on a measure whose
      long-run half (the two-to-three-year reversal) needs several
      non-overlapping multi-year windows to say anything at all. This family
      cannot test that half and does not claim to.
  (2) The source itself reports realized FIT's spread as flat over the
      following year (section 2). Six of the twelve specs are therefore
      expected, by the paper, to come back near zero.
  (3) A 60-day publication wall between the data and any trade.
  (4) A large-cap universe, published 2012, on a mechanism that has had
      fourteen years of public life.

An honest negative is the expected outcome and is a useful one. The failure
mode this pre-registration exists to prevent is not a negative — it is a
positive manufactured by choosing the PSF, the flow definition, the cost model
or the holding period after seeing which one worked.
