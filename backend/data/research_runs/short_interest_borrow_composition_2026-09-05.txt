THE ARITHMETIC, CHECKED RATHER THAN TRUSTED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The schedule is a two-rate step function, so bookbp must be recoverable by hand from three
published numbers and one measured share. tailN is the share of SIR-COVERED short notional
in a tail; the rest of the short side has NO short-interest observation and takes
borrow_cost's documented NaN->GC default. So:

    bookbp  =  cov * [ tailN*430 + (1-tailN)*34 ]  +  (1-cov) * 34

with cov = short-side notional carrying a SIR. Any residual is the gap between a mean of
per-formation products and a product of per-formation means, and is expected to be small.

WHAT THIS RUN DOES *NOT* ESTABLISH -- the limits, stated rather than found later
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. IT IS STILL NOT A BORROW-RATE FEED. Every rate here is D'Avolio's or Beneish/Lee/Nichols',
    mapped onto names by a short-interest proxy both papers explicitly warn against (D'Avolio
    p.285: "the limited use of this measure as a proxy for short-sale constraints"). The
    paid-feed gap stays open -- PENDING_PAID_DATA_DECISIONS.md, entry P1.
 2. THE SCHEDULE OVERCHARGES BY CONSTRUCTION. Beneish/Lee/Nichols p.17: "even in the highest SIR
    decile, less than 30 percent of the stocks are special". Charging a whole decile the
    specials rate therefore prices roughly 70% of it too high, and that bound bites HARDEST on
    this family's long_short specs, whose short leg is entirely inside that decile by
    construction. Their 430 bp/yr is the schedule's answer, not an estimate of what a broker
    would charge.
 3. PERCENTILES ARE WITHIN THE S&P 500 CROSS-SECTION, not the whole market. D'Avolio's deciles
    are market-wide and p.273 says index constituents "are almost always general collateral", so
    an S&P 500 top decile holds far fewer true specials than the market-wide one his fee
    schedule was measured on. Same direction: overcharges.
 4. FINRA's free file starts 2017-12-29 and this family's window opens 2018-01-12, so coverage
    is near-complete here -- unlike lazy_prices, where 7 of 24 formations predated it. Any
    formation reported as uncovered above is excluded from every average rather than folded in.
 5. THE STALENESS IN borrow_cost.py APPLIES UNCHANGED. Its samples end 2013; securities lending
    has since electronified and compressed, which makes both legs of the schedule more likely
    overstatements than understatements in 2026.
 6. THE HEDGED BOOK'S SHORT SIDE IS AN EQUAL-WEIGHTED INDEX BASKET, which is exactly the
    population D'Avolio p.273 calls almost always general collateral -- so its measured rate is,
    if anything, an overstatement of an already-cheap side. The schedule cannot see that,
    because it prices the tails of whatever cross-section it is handed.
