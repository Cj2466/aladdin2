## 2. The finding that matters most — the certifiability ceiling

`sourcing_power_check`'s standard error is √(periods/n) = √(1/years): sampling frequency and
breadth cancel, so only calendar years, `n_local` and the claimed NET Sharpe decide. With the free
data we hold, the minimum claim that returns PROCEED (fractions 1.0 and 0.5) is:

| data window | n_local 5 | 8 | 16 |
|---|---|---|---|
| US equity, Alpaca 2016 → (10.7 y) | 2.25 | **2.42** | 2.63 |
| crypto spot, 2017-08 → (9.1 y) | 2.44 | 2.62 | 2.84 |
| crypto futures/OI, 2020-09 → (6.0 y) | 3.00 | 3.22 | 3.50 |

The best NET microcap claim in the literature read today is 0.93 (Novy-Marx & Velikov Table 13,
PEAD in the microcap bin, t = 6.56 over 49.5 y). Nothing of published-anomaly size passes; the
grounds that would (liquidity provision, arbitrage with hundreds of bets/yr) were found
structurally closed or extinct for a solo retail participant in the 09-11 review and today's
measurement. **The pipeline, at the 0.95 bar and ~10 years of data, cannot certify any single
"small edge" of the kind the project's goal describes.** This is the same fact as the 09-09
criteria audit ("0/50 families had 80% power at true Sharpe 0.5"), now closed from the sourcing side.
