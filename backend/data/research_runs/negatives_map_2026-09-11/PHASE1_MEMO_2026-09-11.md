comes from `dsr_power.dsr_power_report` (itself already validated against

this is NOT a new invention, it is `letf_rebalancing_eod`'s own precedent
formula (POWER_BLOCK.md section 4, "this project's own choice, not a formula
from any source paper"), reused rather than re-derived, and cited as such in
the module docstring.
