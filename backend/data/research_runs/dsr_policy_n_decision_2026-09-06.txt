WHAT WAS ASKED
--------------
Decide what the actual N values in this project's DSR ladder should be. The
ones in use were unjustified. CLAUDE.md section 4 binds the ANSWER SHAPE — a
range with a two-tier verdict, never one number — so this is a decision about
which numbers populate the range, not about collapsing it.

WHAT WAS IN USE, AND WHY IT WAS WRONG
-------------------------------------
Policy D reported DSR at {n_local, 481, 857}.

  481  = global_effective_n.json's `n_specs_clustered`
  857  = global_effective_n.json's `raw_pooled_distinct_trials`
  30   = appeared in the 2026-09-04/05 sensitivity tables

Neither 481 nor 857 was ever chosen as a denominator. Both are PROVENANCE
fields on a MEASUREMENT artifact — 481 means "how many specs happened to carry
a usable realized return series when the pooled ONC run was assembled", an
artifact of data availability, and 857 was that run's raw population count on
2026-09-04. registration_scorecard.py read them directly and thereby promoted
two bookkeeping numbers into the project's multiple-testing correction. THAT
ACCIDENTAL PROMOTION IS THE ACTUAL DEFECT, and it is why the replacement lives
in its own policy artifact (dsr_policy_n.json) rather than being written back
into the measurement one.

CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF: "30" was NOT an arbitrary probe point. It is
variance_effective_n = 29.9008 over the pooled matrix (reproduced this session;
see global_effective_n_dsr_before_after_2026-09-05.txt line 64, "N=30 ...
variance N_eff over the pooled matrix"). It is retired for a better reason than
arbitrariness: effective_n_clustering.py's own docstring says that statistic
"is a RISK statistic, not a breadth statistic... says nothing about whether the
N forecasts carry independent INFORMATION". The module that defines it forbids
this exact use.

PART 1 — WHY THE CLUSTERING DEGENERATED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Answer: it is a FIXABLE-IN-PRINCIPLE OBJECTIVE FAILURE, not an absence of
structure and not a data-sparsity problem. Three findings, all measured this
session from the committed 7334 x 481 return matrix.

(1) SPARSITY IS REFUTED. Of the 115,440 spec pairs, ZERO fall below
    MIN_OVERLAP_FOR_CORRELATION = 60. The smallest pairwise overlap is 1365
    observations. No correlation cell was ever filled by the paper's
    fillna(0). The matrix ONC clustered was fully populated with real
    correlations. The hypothesis that trials are "too sparse/non-overlapping
    to expose correlation structure" is false.

(2) THE STRUCTURE IS THERE.
        within-family  mean |rho| = 0.4676   (5,389 pairs)
        between-family mean |rho| = 0.0813   (110,051 pairs)     ratio 5.75x
        |rho| > 0.7:  within 26.3%   between 0.04%
        |rho| > 0.9:  within  8.0%   between 0.00%
        75 eigenvalues above 1; 87 components for 90% of variance.
    Jensen, Kelly & Pedersen (2023, JF 78(5)) calibrate a block structure at
    0.55 within-theme / 0.03 across-theme on real factor returns — the same
    shape, slightly stronger than this project's.

(3) ONC'S OBJECTIVE SELECTED THE WORSE PARTITION. ONC maximizes the silhouette
    t-statistic q = mean(S)/std(S) (LdP & Lewis 2019 Sec 8.1), NOT the mean
    silhouette. Measured on this project's own matrix:

        k      best q     mean silhouette
        2      2.5344     0.2350      <- what ONC returned
        3      2.3646     0.2347
        13     1.1615     0.2513
        28     1.8185     0.3715
        60     2.0071     0.4183
        87     2.0153     0.4422
        120    2.0430     0.4669
        200    1.6393     0.4468

    k=2 maximizes q by having a LOW STANDARD DEVIATION of silhouettes while
    being materially worse on the silhouette itself. So the recorded finding
    "mean silhouette 0.235 <= 0.25, therefore no substantial cluster
    structure" was measured AT ONC's own degenerate answer; the same data
    supports partitions scoring up to 0.4669, inside Kaufman & Rousseeuw's
    0.26-0.50 band. E[K]=2 is additionally overdetermined: k=2 is the
    algorithm's structural floor (`for i in xrange(2,maxNumClusters+1)` in the
    paper's own published clusterKMeansBase).

    A SECOND FINDING THAT CUTS THE OTHER WAY, and which corrects an earlier
    draft of this memo: scoring the TRUE FAMILY PARTITION (each spec labelled
    by its family_key, k=28) gives q=0.7361 and mean silhouette 0.1937 —
    LOWER than k=2's 0.2350. The 0.3715 above is k-means' OWN k=28 solution,
    not the family partition. The correlation geometry therefore carries real
    structure that does NOT line up with family boundaries: there is
    sub-family structure (k=120 outscores k=28) and some cross-family
    structure. This is direct evidence against reading a family count as an
    independent-bet count, and it is why RUNG 2 below is a robustness tier
    rather than an estimate.

WHAT WAS DELIBERATELY NOT DONE: "fixing" ONC by swapping its objective to the
mean silhouette. That would be a deviation from the cited paper invented to
make the answer come out differently — the opposite of this project's
mechanism-fidelity rule. effective_n_clustering.py is left implementing the
paper as written; the ladder routes around it using estimators that do not
depend on k-means finding a partition.

PART 2 — WHAT THE LITERATURE ACTUALLY SAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* THE DSR PAPER ANSWERS THIS ITSELF, and the answer had not been used.
  Bailey & Lopez de Prado (2014), "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio", Appendix A.3
  "ESTIMATING THE NUMBER OF INDEPENDENT TRIALS" (p. 14), verbatim: "It is
  critical to understand that the N used to compute E[max{SR_n}] corresponds
  to the number of independent trials." It gives a closed-form, CLUSTERING-FREE
  estimator with no k=2 floor:
        Eq. (8)  rho_bar = 2 * sum_{i<j} rho_ij / (M(M-1))
        Eq. (9)  N_hat   = rho_bar + (1 - rho_bar) * M
  Applied to this project's real matrix: rho_bar = 0.031430, N_hat = 465.91 of
  M = 481 (96.9%). Applied to the full raw pool: N_hat = 998.63 of 1031.

* Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado & Zhu (2014), Notices of the AMS 61(5),
  Proposition 2.1, verified: E[max] ~ (1-gamma)Z^-1(1-1/N) + gamma*Z^-1(1-(1/N)e^-1),
  with the stated upper bound sqrt(2 ln N). This is exactly the formula in
  deflated_sharpe.expected_max_sharpe_under_noise (re-read this session; the
  file's sha256 a59c99e1593d... still matches its dependency-manifest pin, i.e.
  the formula file is UNCHANGED). The same paper says treating all trials as
  independent "leads to a quite conservative estimate" — a direct citation for
  the top rung — and recommends PCA where trials are dependent.

* Harvey, Liu & Zhu (2016, RFS 29(1)): their 316 is a count of FACTORS from 313
  papers — roughly one per published economic idea, not per specification.
  Critically for this project's hypothesis, they ran essentially the proposed
  haircut as a robustness check, verbatim: "if you think we are overcounting at
  316, consider taking a haircut to 113 factors... our main conclusions do not
  materially change... Holm at 113 factors is 3.29... while Holm at 316 factors
  is 3.64." A 2.8x reduction in N justified by economic grouping moves the
  hurdle 0.35 t-units. They also estimate 71.1% of tried factors go unreported,
  putting total tests at 824 to >5,000 against 316 published.

* Halle, Djurovic, Andreassen & Langaas (arXiv:1612.04535) — THE CAVEAT THAT
  SHAPED THE LADDER. By simulation, "the methods of Li and Ji (2005) and
  Galwey (2009) do not control the FWER at level alpha = 0.05", while Cheverud
  (2001) and Gao et al. (2008) are conservative. So the eigenvalue estimators
  split into an anti-conservative pair and a conservative pair — which is
  precisely a measured lenient/strict bracket, and is why this ladder uses one
  of each rather than picking a winner.

* Andrew Chen (arXiv:2204.10275): t-hurdles are WEAKLY IDENTIFIED under
  publication bias — "values of piF ranging from 0 to 2/3 imply almost
  identical distributions in the region in which data is well-observed. As a
  result, empirical studies may say little about the proper t-hurdle." This is
  positive statistical support for CLAUDE.md's report-a-range rule, rather than
  the rule being merely cautious.

THE PROJECT'S OWN HYPOTHESIS — VERDICT: SUPPORTED AS STRUCTURE, NOT LICENSED
AS A DENOMINATOR. Between-family correlation genuinely is far lower than
within-family (measured 0.0813 vs 0.4676; JKP find the same shape). But:
  - DSR Eq. (9) would need rho_bar = 0.9650 to deflate this pool to 37. The
    measured value is 0.0314. The pooled average correlation is dominated by
    the 110,051 between-family pairs at mean rho 0.0159, not by the 5,389
    within-family pairs at 0.3490.
  - No paper adopts an economic-mechanism count AS the correction denominator.
    HLZ use grouping as a robustness check; JKP use themes as a Bayesian prior
    structure, not as N, and conclude factors are "strengthened (not weakened)
    by the large number of observed factors".
  - The family partition scores WORSE than k=2 on ONC's own silhouette
    (Part 1, finding 3).
So counting ~38 families is kept as the LENIENT end of the reported range with
a real published precedent, and must never be read as "the" N.

CONVERGENCE OF THE ESTIMATORS ON THE SAME 481-SPEC MATRIX
  DSR Eq. (9)        465.91    ratio to raw 0.969
  Cheverud (2001)    466.47    ratio to raw 0.970   [conservative, Halle]
  Nyholt   (2004)    466.47    ratio to raw 0.970   [conservative, Halle]
  Li & Ji  (2005)    169.01    ratio to raw 0.351   [ANTI-conservative, Halle]
  Galwey   (2009)    152.00    ratio to raw 0.316   [ANTI-conservative, Halle]
  (variance N_eff     29.90 — retired, a risk statistic, see above)
Three independent estimators land within 0.6 of each other at ~97% of raw; the
two Halle-flagged anti-conservative ones land at ~33%. That gap IS the honest
range, and both ends are represented in the ladder.
Cross-check from a different direction: summing each family's OWN internal
effective count gives 189.69 (Galwey) / 191.16 (Li & Ji) against the
whole-matrix 152.00 / 169.01 — same order, the difference being the
between-family correlation the whole-matrix figure correctly nets out.

PART 3 — THE RECOMMENDED LADDER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  RUNG 1   n_local    the family's own pre-declared grid size (9..48).
                      CLAUDE.md's mandated lenient tier. UNCHANGED.
  RUNG 2   37         distinct economic mechanisms searched project-wide.
                      Replaces "30". ROBUSTNESS TIER ONLY (see above).

                      multi_signal_combination (weighted combinations of specs

                      Li & Ji on the real matrix = 169.01, a redundancy ratio

                      LOWER bound on the honest denominator.
  RUNG 4   1031       every distinct (family_key, trial_id) pair persisted.
                      Replaces 857 (the same quantity counted 2026-09-04; the
                      pool has since grown by 174, past global_effective_n's own
                      100-trial staleness threshold). The most conservative N
                      measured, and corroborated within 3% by DSR Eq. (9)
                      (998.63), Cheverud (97.0%) and Nyholt (97.0%).

The eight mechanism merges, each a re-run or second universe rather than a new
search: lazy_prices_{ptit_fix_verification, vocab_ceiling_confound,
xom_hypothesis_test_fast} -> lazy_prices; quality_noa_industry_neutral ->
quality_noa; small_cap_ivol -> ivol; small_cap_quarter_end_marking ->
quarter_end_marking; small_cap_tax_loss_selling_turn_of_year ->
tax_loss_selling_turn_of_year; funding_carry_pit -> funding_carry.

MONOTONICITY, unchanged: DSR = PSR(SR0(N)), SR0 strictly increasing in N, PSR
strictly decreasing in its benchmark, so DSR is strictly decreasing in N and
the four rungs bracket every N between them.

HOW MUCH THIS ACTUALLY MATTERS, stated plainly: sqrt(2 ln N) is 2.72 at N=37
and 3.73 at N=1031. The whole 37-vs-1031 argument is worth about one
cross-sectional standard deviation of trial-Sharpe dispersion. This is a
correctness fix, not a result-changing one — see Part 4.

PART 5 — AUTO-PARK / CONFIG-DRIFT SAFETY CHECK (done BEFORE implementing)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION: SAFE. No live registration's operational status can change as a
side effect of this work. Four independent reasons, each verified directly:

 1. NOT IN THE FINGERPRINTS. config_identity() has exactly six fields —
    cost_bps, min_names_per_leg, financing_bps_per_year, periods_per_year,
    impute_delisting_returns, imputed_delisting_return
    (cross_sectional_forward_registry.py:300-307). spec_identity() has
    thirteen (lines 276-290). NO trial count appears in either. This is the
    exact mechanism by which eac32c9's financing_bps_per_year change DID park
    lazy_prices and short_interest; a denominator is not one of these fields,
    so detect_config_drift and detect_spec_drift cannot fire on it.

 2. THE TICK PATH NEVER RECOMPUTES DSR.
    cross_sectional_forward_validation_runner.py imports no deflated_sharpe and
    contains zero occurrences of "n_trials" or "dsr" (grepped).

 3. family_n_trials IS WRITTEN ONCE, AT REGISTRATION.
    cross_sectional_forward_validation_service.py:196, `family_n_trials=
    adapter.n_trials`, on the CREATE branch only; an existing row is returned
    unchanged (lines 179-186). config_hash is computed from family_key,
    pattern_id, spec_hash and cfg_hash — not from any trial count — so a
    changed denominator cannot even produce a new row.

 4. NO DEPENDENCY-MANIFEST HASH MOVES. global_effective_n.json IS pinned in
    live_registration_dependencies.json's shared_tick_path, and editing it
    would have turned the drift test red and demanded an acknowledgement
    chain entry. THIS CHANGE DOES NOT TOUCH IT. The ladder went into a NEW
    file (dsr_policy_n.json/.py) and the seven edited policy_d_denominators
    modules are not pinned in the manifest by any registration. Verified: the
    manifest's own drift test stays green.
    (Note for a reviewer: a manifest hash move would have been a RED TEST, not
    a status transition — live_registration_dependencies.py explicitly "DOES
    NOT touch any registration's status". But not moving it at all is cleaner.)

NOTHING IN THIS WORK PROMOTES, RETIRES OR WITHDRAWS ANY REGISTRATION, and no
such change is recommended on the strength of it — the verdicts did not move.
