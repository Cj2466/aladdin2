3. WHAT ACTUALLY KILLED IT -- cost per unit of RISK, not cost per trade
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The hedge works. It removes 93.31% of the raw TSMOM leg's variance and leaves a
residual correlated only -0.0316 with the removed component. The problem is
what that leaves behind.

  lb1_hedged       +0.8567   -0.0395   3.90%      2.07x         3.506%

  lb3_hedged       +0.5440   -0.2251   4.00%      2.11x         3.108%

  lb12_unhedged    +0.8176   +0.6796  13.51%      4.15x         1.847%

Hedging barely reduces turnover (18.07/yr netted vs 17.20/yr unhedged -- the
hedge leg adds 13.69/yr of its own, mostly netting against the TSMOM leg) while
cutting volatility by 71%. Cost per unit of trading is unchanged; cost per unit
of RISK roughly triples. The gross idiosyncratic signal is real enough
(+0.71 gross Sharpe); it simply cannot carry a normal futures cost load on a
3.9%-vol book.

THE 1.27x GROSS-UP FIGURE FROM THE PRIOR REPORT DOES NOT TRANSFER -- CORRECTED.
The factor-decomposition report derived ~1.27x from the residual retaining
62.3% of variance. That is an INSTRUMENT-LEVEL retention. At the PORTFOLIO
level the raw TSMOM book is dominated by the common factors, so residualizing
removes far more: measured retention is 8.4707%, and the leverage needed to
restore the raw risk target is 3.4359x, not 1.27x. This is a real correction to
a number carried forward from earlier work, not a restatement.

And the gross-up changes nothing about the verdict, which is worth stating
plainly because it is counter-intuitive: both P&L and cost are linear in gross
exposure, so a uniform gross-up is exactly Sharpe-neutral. Verified
numerically: net Sharpe 0.21719482058874393 at 1x and 0.21719482058874381 at
3.4359x. The gross-up is a risk-target restatement, not a cost the DSR turns on.

5. MECHANISM-FIDELITY REVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FAITHFUL TO SOURCE, cited in code:
  * Ex-ante volatility: MOP (2012) Eq. (1), p.233. Annualization scalar 261
    (not 252), EWMA center of mass 60 days => delta = 60/61, deviation taken
    from the EWMA mean, strictly lagged returns. Transcribed and synthetic-
    validated in tsmom_signal.py (merged bcc7627); imported unmodified here.
  * Position sizing: MOP Eq. (5), p.236, 40%/sigma_t. Note Eq. (5), NOT Eq. (3)
    -- Eq. (3) is the sign predictive regression, and citing it for sizing
    would be a real fidelity error (already flagged in tsmom_signal.py).
  * Diversified portfolio: MOP p.236, equal-weighted 1/S_t over the S_t
    instruments available at t.
  * Headline horizon: MOP p.236, "k=12 and h=1".
  * Lookback grid {1,3,12}: MOP report 1-60 month horizons; Hurst, Ooi &
    Pedersen (2013) build their diversified TSMOM from exactly 1/3/12.
  * Regime rule: pre-registered, and itself honestly flagged in the source
    module as a DERIVED binary convention -- MOP's actual extreme-markets test
    (Table 3 Panel C) is a continuous regression on the SQUARED quarterly
    market return (coef 1.99, t=3.88), not a binary regime.

DEVIATIONS, each logged with reasoning:

  D1. FACTOR-HEDGING INDIVIDUAL TSMOM POSITIONS -- the biggest deviation.
      MOP never proposed this. It exists solely to isolate the idiosyncratic
      breadth this project's own diversification measurement said the raw
      31-instrument universe lacks (breadth 10.0081 vs a floor of 15,
      da504d2). Construction: walk-forward top-6 eigenvector factors, hedged in
      the factor-mimicking portfolios of the same 31 instruments, so the hedge
      is a real tradeable book and not an accounting adjustment. The estimation
      procedure (504-day burn-in, 21-day refit, prior-only standardization,
      eigh + descending sort, OLS on [1, factor scores]) is reused unchanged
      from run_tsmom_factor_decomposition_2026_09_07.py; only the conversion
      back into return units is new, and it is asserted exact along two
      independent routes at every block.

  D2. k = 6 IS NOT SEARCHED. Fixed by the Marchenko-Pastur significance bound
      measured in b8880c8. Sweeping it would search over a quantity the data
      already determined. Noted honestly: the earlier breadth pass was
      k-fragile (the floor cleared only at k in {5,6}).

  D3. REBALANCE EVERY 21 TRADING DAYS, not calendar-monthly as in MOP. The
      hedge legs can only be reset at refit boundaries without lookahead;
      aligning both legs to one grid avoids a second, unpinned calendar.

  D4. FACTOR-MIMICKING PORTFOLIOS ARE NOT DEMEANED. The demeaned version is an
      equivalent variance hedge but carries a phantom cash leg with no
      instrument behind it. The fully-invested version is the tradeable object,
      so the deterministic mean offset stays in the hedge P&L.

  D5. CONSTANT-WEIGHT HOLDING WITHIN A BLOCK. Weights are held at target rather
      than drifting, and cost is charged only at boundaries. This UNDERSTATES
      turnover slightly (a real book drifts and must be trimmed). Flagged as
      favourable to the candidate; it does not rescue it.

  D6. COST NETTING ACROSS LEGS. Cost is charged on the L1 change of the NETTED
      instrument-space book, because the hedge trades the same 31 instruments
      and an offsetting leg is not separately executed. Both leg-wise turnovers
      are reported separately so the netting is auditable.

  D7. CALENDAR-FRONT-MONTH ROLL RULE rather than MOP Section 2.1's most-liquid-
      contract rule -- inherited from the merged chaining work (da504d2), where
      it is disclosed. Not re-litigated here.

  D8. CL NEGATIVE-SETTLEMENT HANDLING. CL settled at -37.63 on 2020-04-20; the
      ratio convention then emits -305.97% and -126.60%, which are artifacts,
      not P&L. Those two rows are masked in the headline (a -305% "return" would
      fabricate an enormous position P&L) and the unmasked panel is reported as
      a sensitivity. Same treatment as da504d2 / b8880c8.

  D9. SHARPE ANNUALIZATION USES 252, while MOP Eq. (1) uses 261 INSIDE the
      volatility model. Both are correct in place: 261 is transcribed from the
      paper for the sizing input; 252 is this project's own reporting
      convention (metrics.TRADING_DAYS_PER_YEAR), kept so this family's Sharpe
      is comparable to every other family's.

JUDGMENT CALLS, flagged rather than buried:

  J1. THE COST MODEL IS THE WEAKEST LINK AND IT IS NOT THIS PROJECT'S EXISTING
      MACHINERY. spread_estimator.py implements EDGE, which needs OHLC; the CME
      SPAN archive gives settlement prices only, so EDGE cannot be applied to
      futures here. MOP (2012) itself was searched in full text and contains NO
      transaction-cost estimate whatsoever, and Baltas & Kosowski (2013)
      explicitly decline to model costs. The anchor used is therefore Hurst,
      Ooi & Pedersen (2013), JOIM 11(3), Section 5 p.55: "the annual
      transaction costs of a Managed Futures strategy are typically about 1-4%
      for a sophisticated trader", footnote 7 (proprietary estimates, ~USD 1bn
      AUM), reference book "run at a 10% annualized volatility". A one-way cost
      per unit notional is BACK-SOLVED so a reference leg pays that annual cost
      after rescaling to its own volatility, and the same figure is charged to
      every spec. This is an industry aggregate, not a measured spread, and it
      bundles commissions and impact with the bid-ask.

  J2. THE REFERENCE LEG MUST BE TURNOVER-MATCHED, and getting this wrong nearly
      produced a false negative. Hurst et al. rebalance WEEKLY (Section 3.1,
      Friday close). Back-solving their annual cost off this book's 21-day
      turnover inflated the per-trade cost by 1.759x and pushed the headline net
      Sharpe to -0.177. The calibration now uses a weekly-rebalanced reference
      leg; the over-punitive version is retained and labelled. Both reach the
      same verdict, but only after the correction is the verdict honest.
      Implied one-way cost: 10.741 bp per unit of notional traded.

  J3. n_local = 6 IS SMALL, and a narrow grid makes rung 1 lenient. It does not
      matter here: the headline net Sharpe is low enough that DSR fails at the
      most lenient rung regardless. sigma_SR = 0.3377, pooled over all six
      specs including both constructions, which INFLATES dispersion and is
      therefore punitive -- flagged as erring against the candidate.

  J4. SHORT SAMPLE. 1623 days (6.4 years). The common window cannot start
      before RTY's 2017-07-10 inception, and 504 days go to burn-in. Any verdict
      on this panel rests on one regime cycle.

  J5. A FLOATING-POINT SIGN TIE was found and inspected, not waved through: ZW's
      21-day window ending 2023-10-26 compounds to +2.22e-16 one way and
      -4.09e-16 the other. MOP offer no tie-break; tsmom_sign returns 0 at an
      exact zero, so a tie is a no-position. The regression check now aborts on
      any disagreement at a material trailing return and tolerates only ties.

  J6. A MIS-SPECIFIED DIAGNOSTIC WAS CAUGHT AND REPLACED. Factor neutrality was
      first measured by pooling the whole history against ONE fixed factor
      basis. That is invalid for a hedge whose basis is re-estimated every 21
      days and whose loadings rotate 31.88 degrees over the sample (d020a3d):
      it reported R^2 = 0.12 for the UNHEDGED leg even though 93.31% of that
      leg's variance is demonstrably in the removed component. Replaced with a
      variance decomposition using each block's own fixed exposures. Recorded
      because the wrong version would have understated the hedge's effect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE BREADTH QUESTION -- why DSR was the decider
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stated plainly, because it is the reason this family was built at all. The raw
universe failed the pre-declared breadth floor (10.0081 vs 15). Residualizing
raised point-in-time breadth to 16.9550, but that estimate was found UNRESOLVED
rather than passing: its bias direction is unidentifiable without already
knowing the true residual correlation shape (4ecd429), and loading drift means
no static-DGP null can settle it (d020a3d). The project owner's explicit
decision was to stop chasing the proxy, build the signal, and let the DSR ladder
decide. That is what happened. The breadth question remains formally open and
this result does not close it -- but it no longer blocks anything, because the
candidate fails on its own returns at every rung of the ladder, under every cost
assumption in the cited band, in both regimes.
