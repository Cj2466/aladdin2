XOM TICKER->CIK RESOLUTION BUG: FIX AND RE-VERIFICATION RESULT
==============================================================
2026-09-02. Companion to xom_cik_fix_PREREGISTRATION.txt, which was
written in full BEFORE the fix was implemented and before any number
below existed. Read that first: it records what was confirmed live,
what would be re-run, and what was predicted.

NOT A NEW TRIAL. This consumes no multiple-comparisons slot. No spec,
holding period, portfolio mode, cost or DSR denominator changed. The
only thing that changed is that one company's real filing history
entered a panel it had been silently excluded from.


1. THE BUG, AS FIXED
--------------------
SEC's company_tickers.json maps a ticker to whichever registrant
currently CARRIES it. Exxon completed a holding-company
reorganization on 2026-07-01 (successor-issuer 8-K12B that day), so
the map moved XOM onto CIK 2115436 "ExxonMobil Holdings Corp" — 29
filings, the earliest 2026-07-01 (25 that day) and the latest
2026-08-28, one 10-Q, ZERO 10-Ks — while the entire
operating history stayed on CIK 34088 "EXXON MOBIL CORP" (3,554
filings from 1994, 10-Ks through 2026-02-18).

edgar_xbrl_provider.py reads 10-K/10-K/A only, so the resolved CIK
produced nothing for every line item in every year. XOM did not land
in the missing-CIK list: the ticker resolved, the fetch returned 200,
and extract_line_items returned empty dicts. Measured on the production
run's own cache, the pre-fix pipeline gave XOM 0 CbOP observations and
0 NOA observations.

CORRECTED BY THE INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION PASS — an earlier draft of
this document said "nothing anywhere recorded it", and that was
overstated in the flattering direction. cross_sectional_quality_neutral
.py section 5 ALREADY named XOM and CIK 2115436 and said its
companyfacts "holds only 10-Qs so far" — and then classified it, with
SE, as one of "TWO current-SIC whole-history fallbacks, both
structurally harmless because neither name can ever rank." So the
resolution WAS observed and written down; what failed was the
conclusion drawn from it, which treated a live mega-cap's exclusion as
equivalent to a foreign private issuer's legitimate one. The pipeline
produced no signal that distinguished the two cases, which is the part
this change fixes — but "no signal" is a weaker and truer claim than
"no record".

THE FIX (edgar_xbrl_provider.EdgarXbrlProvider.resolve_company_facts).
When a resolved CIK's companyfacts carries ZERO annual facts, the CIKs
that issued the accession numbers inside that same already-downloaded
document are probed, most-facts-first and capped at 3, and one is
accepted only if BOTH (1) its own companyfacts carries annual facts
and (2) its entityName matches after case/punctuation normalization.
Otherwise the resolution is refused and reported.
   THE TWO GATES ARE NOT INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE, and it would be
   overselling to present them as such: in the successor-shell case the
   shell's entityName comes from a fact the PREDECESSOR filed, so name
   equality is largely implied by the accession-prefix majority rather
   than confirming it. Gate (2) still does real work — it is what stops
   a filing agent that DOES have companyfacts of its own from being
   accepted — but it is a second filter, not a second witness. Every outcome lands
on EdgarXbrlProvider.cik_resolution and, for the three SCREENING
entry points (run_quality_screening, run_noa_neutral_screening,
run_asset_growth_screening), in their `warnings` list.
   WHAT IS STILL NOT SURFACED, stated rather than implied away: the
   LIVE forward-validation path does not carry the report. Both
   build_cbop_live_panel and build_noa_neutral_live_panel discard the
   provider's accounting, and CrossSectionalLivePanel has no warnings
   field to put it in; multi_signal_combination discards it too. On
   those paths the only announcement is the provider's logger.warning.
   Giving the live panel a disclosure surface is a separate change to
   an in-flight registration's own dataclass and was deliberately not
   bundled here.

WHY "ZERO ANNUAL FACTS" AND NOT A DEPTH THRESHOLD — measured over all
162 companyfacts documents of the 2026-08-28 production run: exactly
two carry zero annual facts (Sea Limited, a 20-F foreign private
issuer that genuinely has none; and the Exxon shell). The
next-shallowest are NINE documents holding 4, 5, 5, 7, 7, 8, 9, 10 and
10 distinct annual fiscal years — every one a genuine recent spin-off
or IPO that should rank on the history it has — against a median of
20. Zero sits in a real measured gap, the same way
ASSETS_SCALE_BREAK_RATIO's 100x sits between 11x and 10,135x.
CORRECTED BY THE SECOND INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION PASS, which
recomputed the distribution from the cache itself: this said EIGHT
next-shallowest documents and named eight companies, dropping Vontier,
which ties Otis at 10 years. Nine, not eight, sit at 10 years or
fewer. The direction of the error flatters nothing — a depth threshold
would refuse one MORE legitimate name than was claimed — but the count
was wrong and is corrected here.

WHY THE ACCESSION-PREFIX SIGNAL IS GATED, NOT TRUSTED — measured on
the same 162 documents: 150 of them contain facts filed under some
other CIK, because filing AGENTS issue accession numbers. The gates
are what does the work: CIKs 1193125, 1144204 and 1140361 (Donnelley,
Toppan) all return HTTP 404 from the companyfacts endpoint, verified
live, so no filing agent can pass gate (1).

REJECTED ALTERNATIVES, each for a stated reason: a hand-maintained
{"XOM": 34088} table (fixes one ticker, would not have found this one,
needs a new entry per reorganization); SEC file-number linkage.
   THE FILE-NUMBER REASON WAS OVERSTATED, corrected by the second
   independent verification pass against the live submissions record.
   What is true: the successor's EXCHANGE ACT file number really is
   001-43384 and not Exxon's 001-02256. What was wrong: the conclusion
   drawn from it, that a file-number linkage "is wrong here". It is
   not — 24 of the successor's 29 filings are S-8 POS amendments
   carrying CO-REGISTRANT file numbers (002-62221-01, 333-279120-01,
   333-293558-01, ...) whose base numbers all resolve to filings under
   CIK 34088, so stripping the -NN suffix WOULD have found Exxon. The
   alternative is rejected on its real merits instead: it costs a
   submissions fetch per ticker where the accession prefixes are
   already in a document on disk; the -NN co-registrant convention is
   an EDGAR habit this project has not verified as a guarantee; and it
   requires the successor to have been co-registered on the
   predecessor's Securities Act registrations, which a reorganization
   with no outstanding S-8 plans need not be.

7. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE IN-FLIGHT LIVE REGISTRATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------
Both families have been registered for forward validation since
2026-08-31 (observational only: ExecutionControl.trading_halted=True,
zero live orders, no capital at risk). Every formation day since then
ranked without Exxon. Deploying this fix therefore creates a DATED
DISCONTINUITY in the live data-generating process for
quality_noa_industry_neutral: formations before the deploy exclude
XOM, formations after include it. quality_cbop is unaffected either
way (XOM is not CbOP-eligible).

The registration's own guards do not object, and that is by design
rather than by luck: QUALITY_LIVE_SAMPLE_FINGERPRINT hashes only the
200 drawn tickers and the 768-name universe size, and XOM was always
in that sample — what changes is only whether the pipeline could
actually reach its filings. Nothing in spec_identity or
config_identity moves either. So no CrossSectionalUniverseDriftError
fires; the change is announced by the provider's WARNING log and by
the families' `warnings` list, not by an assertion.

The reading taken here, stated so it can be argued with: this is a
DEFECT fix that brings the live panel closer to the universe rule the
registration snapshotted (which admits a name on was_member + a finite
close + a non-NaN NOA value + a point-in-time bucket — XOM was being
dropped by a bug, not by that rule), so it should be deployed rather
than deferred to a re-registration. The measured cost of being wrong
about that is bounded and small: the effect on the family's whole
backtested grid is at most 0.034 Sharpe, and the family claims no
validated edge in either arm. WHOEVER MERGES THIS SHOULD RECORD THE
DEPLOY DATE alongside the two registrations, so the forward record is
readable as two segments rather than one.


8. KNOWN LIMIT THIS FIX DOES NOT CLOSE
--------------------------------------
Once the ExxonMobil Holdings successor files its OWN first 10-K —
expected around February 2027 — CIK 2115436 will carry annual facts,
the zero-annual-facts trigger will stop firing, and XOM's history will
truncate to that single year. Handling that needs a MERGE of
predecessor and successor facts rather than a redirect, and the
trigger for a merge cannot be "zero annual facts". This population
gives no measured basis for the threshold such a rule would need, so
it is recorded here and in the provider's own KNOWN LIMITS rather than
guessed at now. It is a dated, foreseeable regression, not a surprise.

SECOND, SEPARATE INSTANCE OF THE SAME ROOT CAUSE, NOT FIXED HERE.
edgar_filing_text_provider.py builds its own ticker->CIK map from the
same SEC file with the same dict comprehension, and
build_filing_index lists PERIODIC_FORMS = ("10-K", "10-Q") from the
submissions API under whatever CIK that map returns. For XOM that is
CIK 2115436, whose live submissions document carries exactly one 10-Q
and zero 10-Ks — so the lazy_prices family (Cohen, Malloy & Nguyen
year-over-year 10-K text similarity) has no XOM filing pair to diff at
all. Confirmed against the live submissions JSON on 2026-09-02;
NOT changed and NOT re-verified in this branch, because it is a
different provider, a different family, and deserves its own pass.
   RE-CONFIRMED END TO END by the second independent verification pass
   on 2026-09-02, one level deeper than the submissions JSON:
   EdgarFilingTextProvider().build_filing_index(["XOM","AAPL"],
   forms=("10-K",)) returns 0 filings for XOM and 25 for AAPL, and its
   FilingIndexReport says n_tickers_cik_resolved=2, n_tickers_indexed=2,
   unresolved_tickers=[], failed_tickers=[]. So the family's own
   accounting surface counts XOM as SUCCESSFULLY indexed with nothing in
   it — the identical silent-exclusion signature, still live, still
   undisclosed in lazy_prices_2026-09-01.txt.
