Re:   Revision to the criteria of Securities Eligible for Short 
To:   Managing Directors  
 All members and non-member securities companies 
Enclosure:  Notification of the Stock Exchange of Thailand Re: Securities Eligible for Short  
(No.7) B.E. 2568 (2025) 
 
The Stock Exchange of Thailand (“SET”) has reviewed criteria of eligible stock for short-selling by 
limiting them to securities with large market capitalization and high liquidity to reduce price fluctuations. 
The revision shall enhance supervision measures and boost investor trust and confidence. These 
amendments will be effective from April 16, 2025. The details are as follows: 
 
Securities Short Selling List 
Common Stocks  
• SET100 / (Remain) 
• Large Market Capitalization with Liquidity as specified 
by SET (non-SET100) 
X (Cancelled) 
• Underlying DW, ETF, Single Stock Futures / (Remain) 
Other Products  DR, ETF (Remain) 
Please be informed accordingly.
