Add the Vol-Regime cross-asset implied-vol timing family (honest negative result)

A 48-spec family (8 vol-index state variables x 3 holding periods x 2
targets) timing SPY-vs-IEF and HYG-vs-IEF off an external, options-derived
volatility state variable. Its own backtest loop rather than a
cross_sectional.py family: the state variable is one scalar per DAY
belonging to no ticker, and the traded object is a fixed spread whose size
and sign it sets -- there is no cross-section to rank. sharpe_ratio and
compute_deflated_sharpe are reused unmodified.

Re-verified the scout's ticker list rather than trusting it, and five of the
nine claimed indices do not survive: ^VIX3M, ^VIX6M, ^VXD, ^VIX9D and ^VIX1D
return exactly one row (today's quote) through both yf.download and
Ticker.history(period="max"). Any VIX term-structure mechanism is therefore
not buildable on free data. ^VXN (2001, 6436 rows) is a genuine addition the
scout missed. Seven usable indices; common formation window 2008-05-13 to
2026-08-25, which captures the autumn 2008 crisis.

Two scout claims that DID reproduce: corr(log(MOVE/VIX), trailing realized
SPY vol) = -0.388 against VIX's own +0.866, so the ratio is not realized vol
relabelled; and MOVE/OVX/GVZ daily changes correlate only 0.32/0.41/0.41
with VIX. One correction: ^VXN (0.919) and ^VVIX (0.822) are NOT independent
of VIX and are barred from the composite.

Non-overlapping formations throughout (cadence == holding_days), per the
review's amendment 2 -- the scout's overlapping-window R^2 methodology is not
carried forward. A circular block bootstrap with block length = holding_days
is reported alongside every spec, because Sharpe still uses the daily series.

RESULT: cleanly negative, and reported as such.
  - best raw Sharpe +0.232 (vol_vxn_vix_h21_spy_ief), max DSR 0.486, no spec
    at or above 0.95
  - min bootstrap p 0.114; none below 0.05
  - Sharpe mean +0.0002, median -0.0071, 23 of 48 positive -- a coin flip
  - E[max Sharpe | 48 zero-edge trials] = +0.240, ABOVE the observed +0.232;
    Monte Carlo puts P(best-of-48 noise >= observed) at 0.9997
  - the family's own claim is refuted by its own contents: the best spec is
    the non-cross-asset ^VXN one, and cross-asset specs (max +0.164) barely
    separate from the vix_level control (max +0.128)

Confound checks run in-module for every spec, not deferred to a later
adversarial pass: beta-hedged residual Sharpe against the buy-and-hold
target spread, equity and rates betas, mean position, and subperiod Sharpes
in thirds. Building these caught a real bug worth recording -- residual
Sharpe was first computed from the OLS residual WITH the intercept, whose
mean is exactly zero by construction, so it would have read ~0.000 for every
strategy ever measured and branded all 48 specs disguised static tilts with
total confidence. It is now the beta-hedged stream y - beta*x. A second fix
in the same area: the static-tilt detector compares signed, not absolute,
because a near-constant position leaves only the cost drag whose Sharpe is
large and negative -- on abs() the most blatant tilt would have been cleared.
Both have regression tests.

n_trials = 48 covers this family only and not the exploration that selected
the hypothesis, so every DSR here is an upper bound. Stated in the module,
in build_vol_regime_disclosure's output, and tested.

Full suite: 999 -> 1091 passed, zero regressions.
