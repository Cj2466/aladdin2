CANDIDATE: cross-sectional idiosyncratic-volatility anomaly.

Citations: Ang, Hodrick, Xing & Zhang, "The Cross-Section of Volatility and
Expected Returns" (Journal of Finance, 2006) — the core result (high
idiosyncratic vol relative to a factor model -> abysmally low subsequent
returns); Bali & Cakici, "Idiosyncratic Volatility and the Cross-Section of
Expected Returns" (JFQA, 2008) — precedent for a single-index (not full
Fama-French) construction, and the source of this proposal's central
caveat; Blitz & van Vliet, "The Volatility Effect: Lower Risk without Lower
Return" (Journal of Portfolio Management, 2007) — the plain-total-volatility
sibling result, used here as an in-family robustness split.

Signal: over a trailing window (21/63/252 trading days, separate
definitions), regress each eligible ticker's daily return on that day's
equal-weighted mean return across the SAME eligible cross-section (r_i,t =
a_i + b_i*r_mkt,t + e_i,t, OLS), then IVOL_i = std(e_i,t). This "market" is
computed entirely inside the signal function from data already in the
harness — no external index fetch — and is the identical equal-weight
construction long_universe_hedged already uses for its short leg, so it's
internally consistent with the rest of Round C, not a new data dependency.
It replaces AHXZ's Fama-French three-factor residual with a single-index
residual: SMB needs point-in-time market-cap history and HML needs
book-to-market, a fundamentals input this project does not reliably have —
a disclosed simplification with its own literature precedent (Bali-Cakici
test single-index IVOL directly), not silent data substitution. Long the
bottom decile (lowest IVOL), short the top (highest IVOL) — AHXZ's own
direction. Requires only close prices: no open, no volume — the cheapest
family this project has specified.

Construction: deciles and quintiles, long_short and long_universe_hedged,
holding horizons 21/63/126 trading days (this round's existing convention).
One within-family robustness split: swap std(e_i,t) for std(r_i,t) (raw
total volatility, Blitz-van Vliet's simpler construction) at the same
horizons, to separate "compensation for undiversifiable firm-specific risk"
(AHXZ's story) from "the market overpays for beta/size exposure generally"
(the simpler story) — same signal_fn shape, one line different.

Why this isn't a repeat of the already-tested patterns: 52-week-high
nearness and capital-gains overhang both rank price LEVEL against a
reference point (anchoring/disposition-effect stories); overnight-intraday
persistence ranks return SIGN. IVOL ranks return DISPERSION and is
direction-agnostic — a stock can have high IVOL with zero net drift. Its
proposed mechanism is limits-to-arbitrage (idiosyncratic risk can't be
diversified away costlessly, so risk-averse arbitrageurs underinvest in
mispriced high-IVOL names), unrelated to anchoring or disposition selling;
AHXZ report their sorts are close to orthogonal to size/BM/momentum sorts.

Honest weakness: this anomaly replicates far less reliably than the others
cited in this project. Bali & Cakici (2008) show the AHXZ result weakens or
reverses under value-weighting, alternate breakpoints, or monthly-sampled
(vs. daily-sampled) vol estimates — equal-weighting (this harness's only
mode) is the specification likeliest to preserve it, which is itself the
fragile-looking part. Separately, Bali/Cakici/Whitelaw ("Maxing Out," JFE
2011) show IVOL predictability is substantially subsumed by a stock's single
maximum daily return in the formation window (lottery demand) — a distinct,
confoundable mechanism this family does not disentangle. And AHXZ's cleanest
result uses a ~1-month formation-to-holding lag, exactly the horizon this
project's cost work says gets destroyed; whether it survives at 63/126-day
holds is the genuinely open question this candidate exists to answer, not
an assumed outcome.

Rejected alternative, stated explicitly per the data-feasibility
instruction: Novy-Marx gross profitability and Asness-Frazzini-Pedersen
Quality-Minus-Junk both require COGS/revenue/book-equity fundamentals this
project has no reliable feed for. No OHLCV-only proxy for either exists that
isn't just re-deriving momentum or this low-vol signal under another name —
so quality/profitability is excluded this round on stated data grounds, not
quietly assumed.
