------------------------------------------------------------------

CrossSectionalConfig(impute_delisting_returns=True) -- Shumway imputation ON.
Reasoning: D2's long leg is, by construction, the extreme past LOSERS
(signal is negated trailing cumulative return), which is exactly the
sub-population most likely to delist mid-hold via distress/bankruptcy.
The harness's generic default (silently dropping a delisted name at its
last price) would understate exactly the risk this family's long leg is
adversely selected for, flattering its Sharpe. This matches the module's
own audited design and was not second-guessed.

MISSING TICKERS -- 144 REPORTED, WITH ONE VERIFIED FALSE NEGATIVE
------------------------------------------------------------------
summary.missing_price_data reported 144 tickers unresolved out of a

Independent verification (mandatory per standing rule): I did not take
"missing" at face value. I retried all 144 tickers individually and
serially (bypassing the multi-ticker batched yf.download call the
production run used) against the same window:
  - 143/144 still failed to resolve, confirmed genuinely unavailable.
  - 1/144 (WFC -- Wells Fargo, a large, currently-active, highly liquid
    S&P 500 constituent) resolved cleanly on individual retry (2925 rows,
    including a fresh 2026-08-25 close), and single-ticker downloads of
    AAPL/MSFT confirmed the yfinance API itself was not broadly down at
    the time. WFC's batch-call failure is a transient artifact of yfinance's
    threaded multi-ticker download (observed here, not reproduced on
    retry), not a genuine data gap.

This means: the production run's 144-missing figure overstates the real
coverage gap by exactly 1 name. The genuine gap is 143 tickers, all of
which are point-in-time-universe members that are NOT current S&P 500
constituents (confirmed departed, e.g. AABA/Altaba, MON/Monsanto,
CELG/Celgene, RTN, UTX, XLNX, CERN, ATVI, ALXN, TIF, PXD, FRC, SIVB and
similar -- all consistent with real M&A/failure delistings, not live
tickers).

Denominator check against the documented ~48% departed-member gap:
  - Total point-in-time universe (2015-01-07 -> today): 768 tickers
  - Current S&P 500 constituents within that universe: 503
  - Departed (in the universe but not a current member): 265
  - Of those 265 departed names, 143 are unresolvable on yfinance
    -> departed-member miss rate = 143/265 = 54.0%

54.0% is the same order of magnitude as, and moderately above, the
previously-documented ~48% figure -- consistent, not a red flag; the
exact rate will drift somewhat between rounds as the universe window and
the specific departed-name mix shift. This is the expected, already-known
yfinance survivorship-coverage limitation, not a new problem introduced by
this run.

Practical impact of the WFC false negative on this result: negligible.
WFC is 1 name out of a 768-ticker universe with ~89-90 names per leg on
average; its inclusion could not plausibly move the Sharpe by enough to
change any of the four already-clearly-negative results. Flagged here as
a known minor data-pipeline quirk (batched yf.download occasionally drops
a valid, currently-active ticker that a serial per-ticker retry recovers)
worth being aware of for future rounds that rely on missing_price_data for
precise arithmetic -- not something that changes this round's conclusion.
