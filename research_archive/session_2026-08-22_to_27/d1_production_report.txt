VALUE-WEIGHTING FALLBACK RATE (worth flagging plainly, not just the summary
the module docstring asks for)
  Across all 21 results: 2,321 total value-weightable legs, 2,011 fell back
  to magnitude weighting = 86.6% fallback rate. Structurally expected, not
  a bug: get_shares_outstanding's per-ticker share-count series only goes
  back as far as yfinance's SEC-filing history for that name, and
  build_point_in_time_market_cap forward-fills only -- no backward-fill --
  so any formation date before a leg member's first known share-count
  observation falls that WHOLE leg back to magnitude weighting (see
  cross_sectional_ivol.py's own FALLBACK DISCIPLINE section and
  build_point_in_time_market_cap docstring). With the screening window
  starting 2015-01-07, a large share of early-window formations across an
  11+ year history landing before some member's earliest resolvable filing
  is exactly what this mechanism predicts. Net effect: this family is, in
  practice, mostly magnitude-weighted rather than truly value-weighted --
  a genuine data-availability constraint on how faithfully it tests the
  Bali-Cakici value-weighting hypothesis specifically, separate from the
  headline negative-Sharpe result itself.
