Add the buyback/dilution (net share issuance) cross-sectional family

Ranks the point-in-time S&P 500 cross-section by the trailing change in
split-adjusted shares outstanding -- long the shrinkers (buybacks), short
the growers (dilution) -- per Pontiff & Woodgate (2008), Ikenberry/
Lakonishok/Vermaelen (1995), Daniel & Titman (2006) and Fama & French
(2008). 14 pre-declared definitions: 3 lookbacks x 2 holds x 2 portfolio
modes = 12, plus 2 winsorized robustness variants at the 504-day lookback.
Asserted at import time against the grid arithmetic, so a size drift is a
loud failure rather than a silent change to the DSR denominator.

Reuses cross_sectional.py's harness whole -- this is a cross-sectional
ranking signal, the same shape as every family since Round C. The one
harness change is a new optional CrossSectionalData.shares_outstanding
frame plus CrossSectionalSpec.requires_shares_outstanding, added exactly
the way price_only_close was for the bonds family: default None/False, so
every existing spec replays byte-for-byte identically (asserted).

TWO REAL DATA DEFECTS, both handled, both re-verified live rather than
inherited from the scouting note.

(1) Splits contaminate raw share counts, and for a signal that IS the
    share-count ratio that is a sign error large enough to invert a decile.
    Confirmed on real tickers: GE's 1-for-8 reverse split (2021-08-02)
    makes its uncorrected one-year change read -87.5% -- log(8) = +2.08,
    literally rank 1 of 561, the largest "buyback" in the index, maximum
    long weight. ANET's 4-for-1 (2021-11-18, count switching five days
    late) reads +300% dilution, rank 559 of 561, maximum short weight.
    Across 26 quarterly formations the correction moves 436 of 2,838 leg
    slots (15.4%). Fixed by reusing Build D1's own fix --
    cross_sectional_ivol.split_adjust_share_counts (commit aad5bf8) --
    imported, not reimplemented. Its "no jump found -> no adjustment" rule
    also correctly leaves alone the three spin-off ratios Yahoo records as
    GE splits (Wabtec 1.04, GE HealthCare 1.281, GE Vernova 1.253).

(2) The series is stepwise with wildly uneven density (median observations
    per ticker per year: 1 in 2015, 5 in 2016, 4 in 2017, 107 in 2019, 146
    in 2024; ANET has zero rows in all of 2022). So: forward-fill only,
    never interpolate; refuse a count carried past 400 days; and -- the
    part that only showed up once measured -- refuse a window whose two
    endpoints are the identical filed count. Those read an exact 0.00%
    "issued nothing" from "reported nothing", and on 2018-06-07 that was
    530 of 542 names at the 126-day lookback, i.e. a "542-name decile
    sort" that would really have been decided by the dozen names that had
    filed. Measured cost of refusing them: mean names ranked 538 -> 527,
    and the 27 formation-days it costs all fall inside the 2018-11..12
    coverage hole.

Also corrects the scout's floor claim against the full universe: the
~2015-10 floor holds for 587 of 607 priced tickers; the other 20 carry
exactly one stray earlier row each, and nothing here leans on it.

Holding periods are 126 and 252 trading days, with 21 and 63 excluded by
assertion. The signal only refreshes when a new share count is filed
(~4x/year), so a shorter hold pays turnover to re-express a ranking that
has not moved. The run measures this rather than asserting it: at 5.0bp
one-way, nsi_l126_ls_h126 pays 29.9bp/yr against its 252-day sibling's
16.0bp/yr on the same signal.

REAL PRODUCTION RUN (2026-08-27, live yfinance), reported in full: 2,173
trading days, all 14 specs replayed, none dropped. 11 of 14 positive raw
Sharpe, 3 of 14 with DSR > 0.5. Best nsi_l504_ls_h126 at +0.412 raw,
0.598 DSR. Unlike this project's previous rounds this is not a clean
negative -- and it is not an edge either. The Sharpe surface is monotone
in lookback across all four (portfolio, hold) pairs, the winsorized
variants track their plain siblings (+0.400 vs +0.412), and the breakevens
are wide (151.7bp one-way trading cost, 427bp/yr short borrow, against
5.0bp and 0 assumed), so costs are not producing it. But DSR 0.598 is a
~60% point estimate against the best-of-14 bar, each spec has only 9-18
NON-OVERLAPPING formations, the sample is one 8.6-year regime, and the
504-day specs have both the best Sharpes and by far the cleanest data
(0.2% uninformative windows vs 8.1% at 126 days) -- a confound this run
cannot separate. Recorded in the module docstring as "worth a
forward-validation slot, not worth capital".

Tests: 63 new, mirroring the bonds/FX conventions, including a regression
that replays the real GE and ANET series inside a 62-name cross-section
and proves each lands in the wrong decile uncorrected and in neither leg
corrected. Suite 999 -> 1062, all green.
